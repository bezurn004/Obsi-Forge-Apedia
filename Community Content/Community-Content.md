# [[_OFA-INDEX|OFA]]: Community Content
Work is progressing to import community content from published material and content posted on discord. As some author's have not permitted full disclosure of their work in the compendium, only permitted portions are included. For the full product see the link to purchase and integrate into your own vault as PDF's.
