# [[_SPCST_EX|Space Sightings Expanded]] - [[_SPCST_EX_CH2_Quest-Front|Quest Front]]: Faction - Coalition of Golden Heralds
*Invoking the New Dawn*

Religious collectives have existed for as long as mankind has been conscious. Since the development of technologies that allowed space travel, and with the discovery of ancient alien artifacts that facilitated colonisation beyond the reaches of our star, the religions of our species have lost influence over the years. However, they never ceased to exist. Their perspective of the cosmos evolved with each scientific breakthrough. Every discovery, every new finding, every advance in human progress has been reinterpreted to reinforce numerous belief systems.

Although the religious factions are as diverse as our people, with the latest war conflicts arising from the Great War, all of them have come to the conclusion that the gods did indeed walk on these celestial bodies we now occupy, and that it is possible to bring them back.

The Coalition of Golden Heralds came together under one banner at the beginning of the Great War for the purpose of countering it. The numerous organizations that stand on faith decided to focus all their efforts on resurrecting an ancestral alien species we call the precursors. To do so, they go on excursions into their vaults. Few return alive, and the ones that do return, do so without much information useful to creating a viable solution to the intergalactic problem.

It is the leader of the Coalition of Golden Heralds who hires you to carry out the enterprise of entering the Supplicant God Nebula, with the purpose of making a step towards humanity’s salvation.

- Faction type: Guild
- Dominion: Mystics and researchers
- Influence: Notable
- Capital: Sigil
- Quirks:
	- Guided by superstition or prophecy
	- Hoards precursor artifacts
- Projects:
	- Fulfill a prophecy
	- Obtain crucial data or information
- Rumors:
	- Caught in the crossfire of feuding factions
	- Knows the location of a fabled treasure or lost technology
- Relationships:
- Holds contempt for the Kamarakon Dynasty and Sacred Accord
- Antagonistic towards the Shattered Flames
