# [[_SPCST_EX|Space Sightings Expanded]] - [[_SPCST_EX_CH2_Quest-Front|Quest Front]]: Faction - Shattered Flames
*Reborn from the ashes*
Not all factions have decided to participate in the Great War— at least not directly. Some, like parasites in a large ecosystem, feed on it and grow from the remains it leaves behind. There are many of them, as numerous as the sectors in conflict, but as is human nature, the lust for power leads some to step on the toes of others, thus carrying out their own wars for power, like scavengers fighting for misery.

One of these factions, Shattered Flames, has gained a dangerously large amount of power in recent years by consuming other fringe groups. Their ships and equipment are composed of the remains of war conflicts, and they’ve not been merciful in dealing with the few survivors of them. For Shattered Flames, there is no ultimate goal for the advancement of mankind. There is only power over their own kind.

Many claim that it has been the rise of this faction that has generated the loss of hope in what is left of humanity, since the two great factions in conflict have not been able to deal with it. This is due to the fact that spies and infiltrators are permeating all the factions allied to the war.

We know that Shattered Flames is nothing more than the pure expression of extremist tribalism, selfish and interested in power for the sake of power. They are governed by the principle that once the war is over, and there are no ideological bastions left to wage it, they will be the victors. And each of the foundations is so engrossed in their own affairs that they are oblivious to this reality.

- Faction type: Fringe group
- Dominion: Pirates and scavengers
- Leadership: Inescapable
- Quirks:
	- Members take a new name when joining the faction
	- Nomadic people and mobile operations
- Projects:
	- Seize a powerful artifact or valuable treasure
	- Obtain a needed commodity
- Rumors:
	- Leaders are puppets of another power or faction
	- Operations are a false front for their true purpose
- Relationships:
	- Apathetic towards the Kamarakon Dynasty, Sacred Accord, and Coallition of Golden Heralds
