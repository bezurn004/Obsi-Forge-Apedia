## Oracle: Starship Registry Number Builder
> [!mechanics]- | Roll > `dice: [[OT_STRSM_OCL_Starships#^Registry-Builder]]` | Guidance
>To build a registry number, roll your challenge dice (2d10) and action die (1d6) twice. The first d6 gives the initializer letters and the second gives your final model letter. The two challenge dice rolls together make up the numbers. For example, rolling 25 (2d10) and 4 (1d6) followed by 81 (2d10) and 3 (1d6) yields the USS-2581-C ^guidance-Registry

| 1d6 | Initializer Letters | 4d10 | Model (1d6) |
| :---: | :--- | --- | :---: |
| 1 | CCV - Custom Command Vehicle | 4d10 in 4 digits | A |
| 2 | RTV - Registered Terminus Vehicle | 4d10 in 4 digits | B |
| 3 | NCC - Naval Corps Contracted | 4d10 in 4 digits | C |
| 4 | USS - United Star System | 4d10 in 4 digits | D |
| 5 | DFX - Deep Forge Experimental | 4d10 in 4 digits | E |
| 6 | ▶Use Faction Initials | 4d10 in 4 digits | J |
^table-Registry
