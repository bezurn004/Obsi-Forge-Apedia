# [[_Starsmith Cultures|Starsmith Cultures]] - [[_STRSM_CUL_CH1_Cultures-Familiar|Familiar]]: Restraint Index
This final value, the most recent addition to the six dimensions, is a measure of happiness within a society that stems from valuing the act of satisfying natural human needs and desires.

**Indulgence Qualities**
- Society tends to be optimistic.
- Freedom of speech is valued and used regularly.
- There is a focus on personal happiness and an emphasis placed on leisure time activities.
- Having many friends is valued.
- People have a perception of personal control over their lives.
- Smiling is viewed as normal.

**Restraint Qualities**
- Society tends to be pessimistic.
- It is believed that personal opinions should be kept to yourself.
- Personal indulgences are regulated and suppressed.
- Doing your job is more important than having friends.
- People feel that life is inflicted upon them and out of their control. Victim mentality is common.
- Smiling is viewed with suspicion.
