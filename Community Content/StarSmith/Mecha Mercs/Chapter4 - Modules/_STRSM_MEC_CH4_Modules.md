## Mech Modules
Just like your Command Vehicle asset, a mech can add modules to further customize your gameplay. Modules attached to a mech can be broken just like modules on the Command Vehicle. Mechs follow the exact same outcomes in [[SF_CH3_Withstand Damage#Withstand Damage Move Card|Withstand Damage]] as your Command Vehicle does except that mechs cannot be cursed.

Modules fall into four categories (weapons, armor, sensors, and utility) which are arbitrary and mean nothing mechanically. They are simply a way to organize them.

Some modules have charges for ammo, chaff, shield energy, etc. which limit their number of uses before having to restock.

Remember that not having a module doesn’t preclude you from attempting the skill described. You just don’t get the bonuses associated with having the module installed.

### Installing Modules
Unlike normal modules, mech modules are only a single ability and each costs 2 experience points to install. Not including the module that comes pre-installed on your particular chassis, each mech has a mod load of 4 meaning you can install up to 4 more modules on your mech. Your mod load can increase to up to 6 if you took the corresponding components in the build phase.

Narratively, installing a module takes time and resources. While you can pay the 2 XP any time, consider how your character would have the time and resources to be able to install a module.

### Replacing Modules
Once installed, you can replace a module given enough time and resources. If you keep the replaced module intact to re-install later, spend an appropriate amount of narrative time making the switch and spending the cost for the new module. The old module can sit in your starship cargo bay waiting to be used again.

**When you want to recycle the old module**, roll +wits. On a hit, take +1 supply and gain one experience point back that must be used immediately for the new module being installed. On a miss, the old module can only be recycled. Take +1 supply.
