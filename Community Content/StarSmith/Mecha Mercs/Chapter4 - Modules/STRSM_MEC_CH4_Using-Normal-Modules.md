# [[_Starsmith Mecha Mercs|Starsmith Mecha Mercs]] - [[_STRSM_MEC_CH4_Modules|Modules]] Using Normal Modules
Many of these mech modules are just single abilities taken from the normal module assets. If you’d rather, you’re welcome to attach a regular module to your mech but it will cost the normal 3 experience points to install and then 2 more experience points each to unlock the next two abilities.

## Creating Your Own Modules
Have other ideas for your mech? Great! With mech modules, you don’t have to think of three abilities to fill out the whole asset. Just focus on one concept and make that. If you think of an idea where there are two or three related abilities, treat each as a separate module with a requirement of having the initial module first.

Remember that you are not bound by any of these rules. They are merely guidelines to help you enjoy the excellent game of *Starforged* in a new way. If you want a wild west mech, go for it!
