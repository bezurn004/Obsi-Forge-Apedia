# [[_Starsmith Mecha Mercs|Starsmith Mecha Mercs]] - [[_STRSM_MEC_CH4_Modules|Modules]] Sensor Modules
These modules either increase your sensor capabilities or improve your hacking.

## Adaptive Subroutines
> When you make a move to hack or disrupt a specific foe, threat, or secure system and miss, set aside a d6 set to a value of 1 known as an invasion die. Until the end of the scene, when you make a move to hack or disrupt the same target, add +invasion die. On a miss, increase the value of your invasion die by 1. On a hit, remove the invasion die.

The more you hack an individual system, the more you discover its weak points thereby allowing you to strengthen your next attack.

## Countermeasures
> Your countermeasure array is armed with 3 chaff. If you [[SF_CH3_Resupply#Resupply Move Card|Resupply]] in a place where your countermeasures can be replenished, you may exchange any earned +supply for +chaff.
> 
> When a threat attacks while they have a sensor lock on you and you [[SF_CH3_React Under Fire#React Under Fire move Card|React Under Fire]], you may fire chaff in an attempt to throw off their targeting matrix. If you do, suffer -1 chaff and treat a weak hit as a strong hit.

The foe thought they had you, but throwing out your countermeasures confuses their ranged targeting system or temporarily blinds their sensors in melee combat. Either way, you turn the momentum of the battle back to your favor.

## Puppet Protocol
> Your mech has aggressive viral coding allowing you to attempt controlling another mech. When you  [[SF_CH3_Secure an Advantage#Secure an Advantage Move Card|Secure an Advantage]] or [[SF_CH3_Gain Ground#Gain Ground Move Card|Gain Ground]] by controlling another mech, add +1. On a strong hit, you may [[SF_CH3_Strike#Strike Move Card|Stirke]] +wits (one time) by directing the enemy mech to attack on your behalf.

Control over a mech lasts as long as narratively appropriate, but you may only [[SF_CH3_Strike#Strike Move Card|Stirke]] once after puppetting. For example, if you’re puppetting the mech to just move out of the way, that may last longer. Generally, the puppet control lasts 1-2 minutes.

## Sensor Lock
> You can  [[SF_CH3_Secure an Advantage#Secure an Advantage Move Card|Secure an Advantage]] by taking the time to lock your sensors on a target. If you do and score a strong hit, for the rest of the scene, add +1 any time you make a move leveraging that sensor lock. The sensor lock is lost when you roll a 1 on the action die on any move against the target. On a weak hit, the same as above but the sensor lock is lost when you roll a 1, 2, or 3 on the action die on any move against the target. You may only have a sensor lock on one target at a time.

Sensor locks let you attack better, hack better, and track better. With a sensor lock in place, you can more easily keep your foe in a defensive position.

## Silent Running
> When you make a move against a specific foe or threat to avoid detection, add +1 and take +1 momentum on a hit.

This module helps you avoid detection with any move. It could be that your energy output from running your mech is much cooler than normal making it hard to see your mech on sensors, a layer of reflective stealth plating that confuses enemy sensors, or engines that are well-oiled and designed for actual silent running.
