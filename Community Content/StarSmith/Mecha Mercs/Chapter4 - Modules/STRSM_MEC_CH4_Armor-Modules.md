# [[_Starsmith Mecha Mercs|Starsmith Mecha Mercs]] - [[_STRSM_MEC_CH4_Modules|Modules]] Armor Modules
These are mainly defensive in nature.

## Deployable Cover
> When you [[SF_CH3_Aid Your Ally#Aid Your Ally Move Card|Aid Your Ally]], [[SF_CH3_Face Danger#Face Danger Move Card|Face Danger]], or [[SF_CH3_React Under Fire#React Under Fire move Card|React Under Fire]] by extending interlocking plates as cover for a friend, companion, or ally, add +1 and take +1 momentum. If your action die shows a 1, mark this module as broken.

The defensive moves of [[SF_CH3_Face Danger#Face Danger Move Card|Face Danger]] and [[SF_CH3_React Under Fire#React Under Fire move Card|React Under Fire]] should make sense here, but to [[SF_CH3_Aid Your Ally#Aid Your Ally Move Card|Aid Your Ally]] with this cover, you should likely be using  [[SF_CH3_Secure an Advantage#Secure an Advantage Move Card|Secure an Advantage]] rather than [[SF_CH3_Gain Ground#Gain Ground Move Card|Gain Ground]] for yourself. In my mind, it’s providing cover for your ally to have time to make their own move of [[SF_CH3_Gain Ground#Gain Ground Move Card|Gain Ground]].

Rolling a one on the action die represents your deployable cover being too beaten up to be usable again in the current battle. It will need to be repaired before it can be used again.

*29 By Eric Bright*

## Energy Shields
> When you raise your shields, roll +your mech’s integrity or +wits. On a strong hit, set your shields to 4. On a weak hit, make them 3. On a miss, make them 2 but  [[SF_CH3_Lose Momentum#Lose Momentum Move Card|Lose Momentum]]  (-1).
> 
> With shields raised, if you [[SF_CH3_Withstand Damage#Withstand Damage Move Card|Withstand  Damage]], ignore damage up to the value of your shields and suffer -1 shields. Raised shields last for a few minutes. If reduced to 0, they cannot be raised again for an hour or so.

This is the first ability from the [[AST_Shields|SHIELDS]] asset ported over for mechs and represent a forcefield of energy being projected from your mechs core.

## Heavy Armor
> When you [[SF_CH3_Withstand Damage#Withstand Damage Move Card|Withstand  Damage]], you may reroll one challenge die.

This comes directly from the original [[AST_Exosuit|EXOSUIT]] asset.

## Inertial Dampeners
> When you [[SF_CH3_Withstand Damage#Withstand Damage Move Card|Withstand  Damage]], add +1. On a strong hit, take +1 momentum.

This module combined with the previous Heavy Armor makes your mech resistant to damage, but remember that you suffer the -integrity of an attack first and then [[SF_CH3_Withstand Damage#Withstand Damage Move Card|Withstand  Damage]] to mitigate the damage rather than flat out negating it.

## Reactive Shields
> When you [[SF_CH3_Withstand Damage#Withstand Damage Move Card|Withstand  Damage]] in a fight from close combat with a foe or threat and score a strong hit, mark progress as your armor lashes out at your foe with destructive energy.

Your mech is wreathed in electrical or fiery energy such that an enemy risks getting hurt themselves when they attack you.

*30 STARSMITH: MECHA MERCS*
