# [[_Starsmith Mecha Mercs|Starsmith Mecha Mercs]] - [[_STRSM_MEC_CH4_Modules|Modules]] Utility Modules
These modules can either be used in a variety of circumstances or aren’t directly a weapon, armor, or sensor boost.

## Arachnos Arms
> Your mech has additional limbs with some that are capable of finer manipulations and some that are meant for entangling foes. Envision how many limbs and their configuration.
> 
> When you  [[SF_CH3_Secure an Advantage#Secure an Advantage Move Card|Secure an Advantage]] or [[SF_CH3_Gain Ground#Gain Ground Move Card|Gain Ground]] by attempting to disarm, trip, shove, grapple, or remove a module from an enemy mech, add +1 and take +1 momentum on a hit.

This mech module comes from the [[AST_Brawler|BRAWLER]] asset. You might have spider-like limbs similar to Spiderman’s in End Game or Dr. Ock’s, or they might truly be humanoid arms. Either way, at least a couple of them have finger-like appendages allowing you to open a normal-sized door or perhaps even hit normal-sized buttons while still within your mech.

## Drones
> Your mech can release a small swarm of drones which function as the [[AST_Combat Bot|COMBAT BOT]] asset with only the first ability unlocked and 3 integrity. You must [[SF_CH3_Repair#Repair Move Card|Repair]] damaged drones as with a normal mechanical companion.

You don’t get the full [[AST_Combat Bot|COMBAT BOT]] asset, but you do get the first ability for cheaper (only 2 experience points as a module here) balanced by the reduction in integrity for the swarm.

## Emergency Repair Nanites
> Once per fight, you can [[SF_CH3_Repair#Repair Move Card|Repair]] by releasing a nanite swarm that reinforces and repair's your armor. If you do, add +1 and treat your result as if you were “In the field” instead of “Under fire.” On a hit, add “Take +2 momentum: 1 point” to your list of options.

Everybody loves a heal bot, right? Remember that while you [[SF_CH3_Repair#Repair Move Card|Repair]], the enemy is likely still active, so alter your narrative positioning accordingly.

## Eva Module
> Your mech is equipped with maneuvering thrusters and pressurized for activity in space or underwater.

This is one module that you should feel free to add to every mech for free if it fits your version of The Forge. If space combat is the main place that mech combats take place, it would make sense that every mech ever built would have the EVA module.

## Flight System
> Your mech can hover mid-air, take flight in the atmosphere with the same maneuverability as a shuttle craft, travel at sub-light speeds when in space, make thrust-assisted leaps, and drop to a surface from altitude.
> 
> When you burn fuel to overcome a critical obstacle (decide after rolling), you may reroll any dice. If you do,  [[SF_CH3_Sacrifice Resources#Sacrifice Resources Move Card|Sacrifice Resources]] (-1).

This module is a slightly modified version of the second ability from the original [[AST_Exosuit|EXOSUIT]] asset. Again, if all the mechs in your world fly, just put this ability on all the mechs you build for free.

## Gel Charges
> Your mech carries 3 explosive gel packs that can be launched from a ranged weapon designed to take out structural supports. If you [[SF_CH3_Resupply#Resupply Move Card|Resupply]] in a place where these specialized explosives can be replenished, you may exchange any earned +supply for +gel pack.
> 
> When you make a move to attack, damage, destroy, or sabotage an object or structure by firing a gel charge, suffer -1 gel pack and roll +edge if you fire it from your weapon or roll +wits if you have time to place it manually. When you roll, roll the action die twice and keep the greater value. Then, if your action die alone is greater than both challenge dice, treat it as a strong hit with a match. If your action die alone is greater than one of the challenge dice, treat it as a strong hit.

This module is a slightly modified version of the second ability from the original [[AST_Exosuit|EXOSUIT]] asset. Again, if all the mechs in your world fly, just put this ability on all the mechs you build for free.

## Grapple Gun
> Your mech can  [[SF_CH3_Secure an Advantage#Secure an Advantage Move Card|Secure an Advantage]] by firing a magnetically charged anchor and tether. On a hit, the grapple finds its target. When you may make a move leveraging this tether, add +1 and take +1 momentum on a hit. The grapple stays anchored until you score a miss on a move leveraging it or you take the time to detach it from your frame. Only one such grapple can be attached at a time.
> 
This is similar to the [[AST_Grappler|GRAPPLER]] asset, but a little simpler in implementation. You could fire this grapple at a ship leaving the area in an attempt to stop them or be pulled by them, grapple another mech’s leg and then circle around them to trip them, or magnetically attach to a door opening away from you in an attempt to keep it closed. You get the idea.

## Radioactive Core
> When you [[SF_CH3_Strike#Strike Move Card|Stirke]] or [[SF_CH3_Clash#Clash Move Card|Clash]] in close quarters while your mech has 2 or less integrity, you may vent radiation from your core (decide before rolling). If you do, mark (an additional) progress on a hit but suffer -1 health on a miss.

Sure it’s fun running mechs hot in the danger zone, but that cavalier attitude can come back to bite you.

## Ram Jets
> Your mech is equipped with a reinforced ramming surface and a short-burst hyper-acceleration jet.
> 
> When you [[SF_CH3_Strike#Strike Move Card|Stirke]] or [[SF_CH3_Gain Ground#Gain Ground Move Card|Gain Ground]] by ramming a foe or obstacle at jet speed, you may roll +integrity. If you do, take +1 momentum on a hit. No matter the result, if the lowest challenge die is greater than your integrity, suffer -1 integrity.

Envision your mech’s ramming surface as a cowcatcher chest, arms that clamp together to form a battering ram, or however you would like. The end result is you slamming into or through objects at insane speeds. Of course, you risk damaging your rig, but that’s worth it if you have a high enough integrity to make the ram a good option.
