# STARSMITH
Thanks for checking out this supplement! Just like I did with the *Ironsmith* supplements, I’ll likely be consolidating any *Starsmith* supplements into a single hard  over copy. The goal is to produce a few more supplements over the course of the 2023 and then put them out again in a single volume in Q1 of 2024.

If you grabbed this supplement for free, great! I hope you get a chance to use this at your table. While I’m not in this for the money, I’d love to be able to save up to get a real book cover for the eventual *Starsmith* book. If and only if you find this supplement fun to use at your table, I’d kindly request you head back to DriveThruRPG and purchases this product again for whatever you feel it’s worth. Thank you!

## DEVELOPMENT REQUESTS
If you have the seed of an idea that you’d like me to explore in the *Starsmith* series, please contact me at ericbright2002@yahoo.com which I’m told is a superb old man email address. As of Dec 24, 2022, potential supplements on my list include:

- **Clandestine Operations:** A supplement about espionage, hacking, and surgical **Strike** missions.
- **League of Allied Planets:** A supplement that puts a large governing body in parts of The Forge with a task of seeking out those far-flung citizens whose Ironhomes went off course. This will discuss playing multi-player with each person in an officer’s role (a la Trek).
- **Cultures Alien and Familiar:** A supplement to help flesh out cultures in different sectors as humanity drifts apart and suggestions for adding aliens to The Forge.
- **Dragon Planet:** An in-depth look at an isolated science-fantasy planet where dragons are critical to survival. This is inspired by The Dragonriders of Pern.

No promises though! Currently, I’m actively working on and most excited about a zombie apocalypse hack that may end up under the Starsmith line but could also be it’s own stand alone product.
