# [[_Starsmith Mecha Mercs|Starsmith Mecha Mercs]]: Mech Stats
## Mech Characteristics
While each mech chassis has unique abilities, remember that you can have your mech operate in any way you choose within the narrative. For example, if you want a Gladiator mech that also hacks, you can do that with fictional positioning, but you won’t get the mechanical bonuses that the Medusa would.

On the mech character sheet (provided in PDF form separately), note any characteristics that you would like your mech to have. These may be purely aesthetic or actual systems that your mech has on board that want narrative access to. All mechs have basic weapons, armor, and sensors, so think about these questions to help you describe your mech.
- What weapon(s) does your mech wield? Are they directly attached to the frame, or can they be holstered or sheathed?
- What armor does your mech rely on? High density alloys, ablative plating, angle-adjusting armor plates that calculate incoming attack trajectories, wings that double as a shield, or a wielded shield?
- What sensors does your mech carry? A small mounted satellite dish array, a laser eye patch that guides sensor signals more precisely to targets, tendrils or wired hookups to gain access to computer systems, air/soil samplers, or a chest-mounted deflector dish for scrambling enemy transmissions?
- What does your mech look like? How big is it? Does it have a recognizable symbol? If so, what does it represent?
- It is assumed that your mech can fold down to a size that will fit on your Command Vehicle, but perhaps you’d rather your mech attach to the outside of the vessel. Does your mech transform into your Command Vehicle like a Gobot? (Seriously, do you remember Gobots? I had almost all the toys before Transformers took them out of action.)

## Mech Stats

Once you have your mech chassis picked out, you’ll next need to choose a method for utilizing stats while piloting your mech.
There are three options:
1. **Use Your Stats** - When you pilot your mech, it’s exactly the same as piloting your Command Vehicle. All your stats are the same and rolls are made like normal. Your other assets interact the same way they would with a Support Vehicle or your Command Vehicle. Give the Gladiator an integrity of six and the other chassis an integrity of five.
2. **Favored Stat Bonus** - Give the Gladiator an integrity of six and the other chassis an integrity of five. When you pilot your mech and make a move with your favored stat that utilizes the mech’s capabilities in some way, choose one option below:
	- Trust the machine: Add +1.
	- Get pumped: Take +2 momentum on a strong hit.
3. **Use Mech Stats** - When you pilot your mech, use the mech stats for all rolls except +heart. In that case, use your character’s heart stat. Since wits is used for hacking, you may use this as an alternate rule:
	- When rolling +wits by utilizing your mech’s onboard computer systems to hack, run sensors, etc., use the lower value between your character’s wits and your mech’s wits.

This is really a preference issue on how you want to play. I like using the mech stats so a character who used iron as their dump stat can go crazy body slamming Andre the Giant like Hulk Hogan in a metal body, but you may want the simplicity of not switching stats throughout gameplay.

### Build Stats From Components
If you choose to use the mech stats, each mech starts with a 1 in all stats except their favored stat which starts at 2. All mechs start with 4 integrity and can install up to 4 modules. To get your final values, you’ll need to finish building your chassis by choosing what components will go into the initial frame construction with each component giving a +1 to a stat, integrity, or module capacity. Choose a number of components based on your preferred style of play.
- For grittier play, choose four components.
- For adventurous play, choose six.
- For heroic play, choose eight.

Choose from the components listed below, but each component is unique. In other words, you can’t pick Frictionless Drive twice, only once. You may mark your chosen components below.

|  | **COMPONENT** | **BONUS** |
| :---: | --- | --- |
| □ | High-Density Frame | +1 Integrity |
| □ | Blackstar Iron Frame | +1 Integrity |
| □ | AI-Target Assist | +1 Edge |
| □ | Frictionless Drive | +1 Edge |
| □ | Redundant Servos | +1 Iron |
| □ | Reinforced Supports | +1 Iron |
| □ | Sensor Scrambler | +1 Shadow |
| □ | Chameleon Nano-Plating | +1 Shadow |
| □ | Cryptographer Database | +1 Wits |
| □ | Processing Booster | +1 Wits |
| □ | Side Mounts | +1 Module Load |
| □ | Rear Mounts | +1 Module Load |

Note that these components have no asset-like abilities attached to them. They are just fancy names to give you a +1 to a stat. You are welcome to use them narratively as you would like, but components (and their stat bumps) cannot be changed after the chassis is built. A new mech must be built to change components.
