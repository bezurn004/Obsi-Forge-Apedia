# [[_Starsmith Mecha Mercs|Starsmith Mecha Mercs]] - [[_STRSM_MEC_CH3_Chassis|Chassis]]: Medusa
The Medusa utilizes its sensor array and cutting edge processors to hack into just about anything. It can find and isolate signals to not only detect
enemy mechs but find the exact frequency and codes it needs to invade that mech’s systems. If you prefer hack to slash, this is your rig.

> ### ASSET ABILITIES
> ⬢ When you make a move to hack or disrupt a specific foe, threat or secure system, add +1 and take +1 momentum on a hit.
> ⬡ When you [[SF_CH3_Secure an Advantage#Secure an Advantage Move Card|Secure an Advantage]] or [[SF_CH3_Gather Information#Gather Information Move Card|Gather Information]] by scanning your surroundings or analyzing a nearby object, choose one (before rolling) and take +1 momentum on a hit.
> 	- Manual scan: Add +1.
> 	- Automated scan: Instead of rolling the action die, make it the value of the Medusa’s integrity.
> ⬡ When you [[SF_CH3_Enter the Fray#Enter The Fray Move Card|Enter The Fray]] against an ambush or [[SF_CH3_Face Danger#Face Danger Move Card|Face Danger]] to detect a hidden threat, you may roll +integrity. If you do, reroll any dice and take +1 momentum on a hit.
> 
> **STARTING MODULE:** Adaptive Subroutines
> **FAVORED STAT:** Wits

The idea behind the Medusa is making a lot of rolls +wits to represent hack attacks or computer system invasions. The physical build of the mech is meant to augment those sorts of attacks so that a hacker in a Medusa would do much better than a hacker in a Gladiator.

The first ability’s trigger is nearly identical to the original [[AST_Tech|TECH]] path asset with the Medusa gaining momentum where the [[AST_Tech|TECH]] gets a reroll. The abilities can be used together to stack the two add +1s on the initial roll.

The second and third abilities are taken from the original [[AST_Sensor Array|SENSOR ARRAY]] module with the idea being that you can use your hacking capability to break through enemy cloaks or decrypt their comms channel.
