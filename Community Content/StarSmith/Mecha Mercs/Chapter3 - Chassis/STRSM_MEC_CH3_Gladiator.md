# [[_Starsmith Mecha Mercs|Starsmith Mecha Mercs]] - [[_STRSM_MEC_CH3_Chassis|Chassis]]: Gladiator
Nothing outlasts the Gladiator chassis (batteries not included)! This frame is designed for power and endurance. It takes a lickin’ and keep on tickin’. If you want to crush your enemies beneath your feet like parents telling their kids that Santa is a lie, you need to pilot a Gladiator.

> ### ASSET ABILITIES
> ⬢ When you [[SF_CH3_Face Danger|Face Danger]] or [[SF_CH3_React Under Fire|React Under Fire]] by taking a hit, crashing through an obstacle, or resisting forcefully, you may (after rolling) replace the value of your action die with the Gladiator’s integrity; if you do, [[SF_CH3_Lose Momentum|Lose Momentum]] (-1).
> ⬡ When you [[SF_CH3_Strike|Stirke]] or [[SF_CH3_Clash|Clash]] with brutal power by overcharging weapons or overloading melee servos, you may add +integrity/2 (rounded up). If you do, count a weak hit as a miss.
> ⬡ When you [[SF_CH3_Secure an Advantage|Secure an Advantage]] or [[SF_CH3_Gain Ground|Gain Ground]] using the staggering strength or resistance of the Gladiator, add +1 and take +1 momentum on a hit.
> 
> **STARTING MODULE:** Heavy Armor
> **FAVORED STAT:** Iron

The three abilities above really split up the original [[AST_Exosuit|EXOSUIT]] asset’s first ability. I felt the original [[AST_Exosuit|EXOSUIT]] was great if you didn’t want a deeper mech, but that first ability was very broad if you want to fine-tune. As much as I love overpowered play (I use 4,3,3,2,2), that one ability was a lot.

The first ability here is defensive in nature and relies completely on absorbing damage or force within the frame of the mech. Hence, the replacement of the action die.

The second ability is a powerful attack buff (especially if you end up with 6 integrity) but it’s risky to overclock those servos.

The third the offensive complement to the first, but since you’re in control, it’s a more measured response of knowing what your rig is capable of. (I ended a sentence with a preposition!)
