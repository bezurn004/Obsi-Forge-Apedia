# [[_Starsmith Mecha Mercs|Starsmith Mecha Mercs]] - [[_STRSM_MEC_CH3_Chassis|Chassis]]: Zephyr
The Zephyr chassis is built from the ground up for speed. It can move swiftly across the battlefield to hold strategic positions, perform hit and run attacks, and even retreat easily if need be. If you have a need for speed, this is the chassis for you.

> ### ASSET ABILITIES
> ⬢ When you [[SF_CH3_Face Danger|Face Danger]] or [[SF_CH3_React Under Fire|React Under Fire]] to navigate through hazardous skies, avoid obstacles, or evade an attack, add +1 and take +1 momentum on a hit.
> ⬡ When you [[SF_CH3_Enter the Fray|Enter The Fray]] by speeding towards a strategic position, you may reroll any dice. On a strong hit, add +1 to your next move.
> ⬡ When you fire your afterburner to [[SF_CH3_Secure an Advantage|Secure an Advantage]] or [[SF_CH3_Gain Ground|Gain Ground]] to perform a risky maneuver, you may add +integrity/2 (rounded up) and take +2 momentum on a strong hit. If you do, count a weak hit as a miss.
>
> **STARTING MODULE:** Flight System
> **FAVORED STAT:** Edge

Notice that the first ability is defensive in nature. In a fight, this can only be used when you are in a bad spot. Outside of combat, this can be used any time you [[SF_CH3_Face Danger|Face Danger]].

The second ability represents you getting where you need to be before your foes. Need to hold the bridge? You can make it there faster than the trudging drones coming for you. Need to make it to the artillery cannon before the gunners get there? You got this!

The third ability (taken from the [[AST_Hoverbike|HOVERBIKE]] support vehicle asset) should be seen as not stacking with the first ability. In the first ability, you’re navigating which implies a more careful approach while this ability implies reckless speed. Damn the warning lights! It’s time to GO!
