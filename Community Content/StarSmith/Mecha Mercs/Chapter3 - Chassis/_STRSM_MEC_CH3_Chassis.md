## Mech Chassis

The initial mech chassis is considered an asset meaning it **costs three experience** to purchase or be taken as a starting asset. For classification purposes, consider the mech asset a Command Vehicle asset because you will be able to add and break Modules.

Remember that building a mech is a complex and lengthy process. If you don’t have the **Mech Printer** asset, make sure your character takes an appropriate amount of time and resources effectively paying a narrative cost.

There are four chassis assets to choose from, each with a different focus for combat that corresponds to a stat. Each chassis not only has unique abilities available through their asset but also a starting module that comes installed on the chassis.
- **Zephyr** (Edge) - Built for speed and flight.
- **Gladiator** (Iron) - Built for strength and endurance.
- **Predator** (Shadow) - Built for stealth and surgical strikes.
- **Medusa** (Wits) - Built for hacking and scanning.

Most of the abilities for these chassis are taken directly from the assets Shawn created for vanilla *Starforged*. (Thanks for the generous licensing, Shawn!) I’ve pulled together multiple abilities that fit each concept I had for a chassis and added tweaks here and there. Each is unlocked for two experience like normal.
