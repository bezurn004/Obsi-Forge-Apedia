# [[_Starsmith Mecha Mercs|Starsmith Mecha Mercs]] - [[_STRSM_MEC_CH3_Chassis|Chassis]]: Predator
The Predator chassis is really the mech version of the Predator alien. Cloak and attack. It’s cloak functions much like the original [[AST_Shade|SHADE]] asset but can recharge during the same scene. Since every ability depends on the cloak, you need it keep working.

> ### ASSET ABILITIES
> By firing up your Harpocratic Reactor, you may instantly cloak yourself in refracted light becoming nearly invisible to both sight and sensors.
> ⬢ When you are cloaked and make a move against a specific foe or threat to avoid detection, you may preset your action die to the Predator’s integrity. If utilizing cover, make it one more (up to six).
> ⬡ When you [[SF_CH3_Enter the Fray#Enter The Fray Move Card|Enter The Fray]] while cloaked, add +1 and mark progress on a strong hit.
> ⬡ When you [[SF_CH3_Strike#Strike Move Card|Stirke]] while cloaked, you may roll +shadow. If you do, choose one (before rolling):
> 	- Strike true: Reroll any dice.
> 	- Strike hard: Mark progress on a hit.
> On a strong hit with a match, you also remain totally undetected; take +2 momentum and add +1 if your next move is a [[SF_CH3_Strike#Strike Move Card|Stirke]].
> 
> On a miss for any of these, in addition to any other cost, you are revealed and can’t cloak again until a new refraction pattern is computed. Set aside (or note) the action die result from the miss. After each move you make, increase the value of that die by one. When the die is at six and you would increase the value, your Harpocratic Reactor resets allowing you to cloak again.
> 
> **STARTING MODULE:** Silent Running
> **FAVORED STAT:** Shadow

Scientists IRL built Harry Potter’s invisibility cloak by bending light around an object. The Harpocratic Reactor works this way, but enemy mechs or sensors can decrypt their visual data by calculating the refraction math. Narratively, when your cloak drops you are either spotted in this way or your cloak was temporarily knocked out by damage.
