# [[_Starsmith Mecha Mercs|Starsmith Mecha Mercs]] - [[_STRSM_MEC_CH2_Assets|Assets]]: Wealth Management
#incomplete _asset card for Wealth Management_

This asset is a way to separate your money (denoted as wealth) from actual resources (supply). If you want wealth to be part of your games right from the start, consider taking this asset for free.

Unlike base Ironsworn where the wealth truly was gold in a bag on your belt, remember that in Starforged you have more options for how you narratively handle wealth. It might be credits on an encrypted chit, digital funds in a bank, investments in stock portfolios, etc. Make it fit your character and their lifestyle.

I tweaked the first ability so that it can overlap with the [[AST_Mercenary|MERCENARY]] asset. That means you could get paid and still earn a higher legacy reward if you have both assets.

The first ability not only allows you to gain wealth but to leverage that wealth. For example, if you [[SF_CH3_Compel|Compel]] someone with a bribe, you can use wealth. Additionally, you can wipe out your wealth (since 6 is the max) to clear the indebted burden. That gives you an idea of the scale of wealth since six buys back your starship. On top of that, I added a fun little narrative way to spend wealth in a “game-changing investment.” What is that? I don’t know! You decide something cool to fit your game like funding research into new technology or helping to rebuild a decimated settlement.

The second ability lets you exchange between supply and wealth, but it’s always at a loss so you don’t cheese the system.

The third ability is very similar to the [[AST_Scavenger|SCAVENGER]] asset’s first ability, but they are meant to be separate based on intent. Are looking for supply or wealth? As written, you could scavenge for supplies and then search for wealth, but I’d recommend deciding on one or the other to roll, not both.

*15 By Eric Bright*
