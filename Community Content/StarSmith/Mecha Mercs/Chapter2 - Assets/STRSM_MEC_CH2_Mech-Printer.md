# [[_Starsmith Mecha Mercs|Starsmith Mecha Mercs]] - [[_STRSM_MEC_CH2_Assets|Assets]]: Mech Printer
#incomplete _asset card for Mech Printer_

Building a mech is a complex feat of engineering. If your character undertakes the task of building a mech without this printer, it could take months to finish building just the chassis if they are building it on their own. Not to mention the amount of resources it would take! In other words, make sure there is a high narrative cost to building a mech without this asset or some other in-game assistance (such as corporate backers donating the mech).

Once you have this printer, you can print a new mech directly onboard your starship. Having this asset also opens up the possibility of paying with wealth rather than experience so that you can save your experience for other assets. (More on wealth later.) On all future pages, I refer to the cost of a mech as experience because not everyone will take this asset and the Wealth Management asset together.

The second ability here gives you the same quick method of producing modules for your mech. Without this, crafting a module could take weeks of building, testing, and installing.

The third ability is a near copy of the third ability on your starship. You are forging an emotional bond to a specific mech that gets a special designation as your primary mech. This represents you getting to know the little quirks and subtle touches necessary to pilot your mech with excellence.
