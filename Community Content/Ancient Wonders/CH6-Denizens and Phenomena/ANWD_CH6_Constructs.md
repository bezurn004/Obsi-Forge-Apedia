# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH6_Denizens-Phenomena|Denizens and Phenomena]]: Constructs
When the time comes for you to venture within the vast inner spaces of a megastructure or megacities, the majority of your encounters will be with the keepers. However, in your adventures throughout the galaxy, it is possible to encounter hostile constructs made by humans. While overall less capable than the precursors' creations, they still present a real threat.

## Oracle: Encountering A Construct
When you are forced to face a construct in a high-stakes, action-oriented conflict and need to determine its rank, follow these guidelines:
- **If you encounter a construct outside a megastructure,** roll or pick from the table below.
- **If you encounter a construct within a megastructure,** follow the **Encounters Rank** guideline provided in Chapter 5 on page 99.

| dice: 1d100 | Result |
| :---: | --- |
| 1 - 20 | **Troublesome**<br>A construct of minimal capability or meager destructive potential |
| 21 - 55 | **Dangerous**<br>A construct of middling capability or limited destructive potential |
| 56 - 80 | **Formidable**<br>A construct of advanced capability or great destructive potential |
| 81 - 95 | **Extreme**<br>A construct of incredible capability or immense destructive potential |
| 96 - 100 | **Epic**<br>A construct of unrivaled capability or unparalleled destructive potential |
^table-Encounter-Rank

Remember to envision how the environment and the nature of your goal may impact the rank of a combat objective. If you are outmatched, make the rank higher. If you are well-prepared or in a position of strength, make it lower.

## Construct Generation
Below are the steps for generating constructs. These are not intended to be used all at once, but as the narrative and fictional circumstances demand.
- **Appearance:** The *Environment*, *Scale*, *Motility*, and *First Look* oracles will flesh out the construct’s appearance.
- **Encounter:** The *Encountered Behavior*, *Purpose*, and *Initial Disposition* oracles frame how an encounter with a construct begins. If unsure how the machine engages in combat, **Ask the Oracle** (50/50) if the construct attacks at a distance. If not, it does so in close quarters.
- **Revelation:** As you interact with a construct and gain a deeper understanding of it, use the *Sentience* and *Revealed Aspect* oracles to get a clearer picture of its nature or reveal new characteristics.
