**Human constructs' disposition**

Instead of rolling, you may choose a construct's purpose or function.

Some of the results in this table may lead you to wonder as to whether an encountered construct has some level of sentience. If you do, roll ==***>Construct Sentience***== to determine whether it does, and envision how you come to this realization.

**Keepers' predisposition**

While the precursors' constructs are generally callous to humanity and creatures outside of the vaults and megastructures that litter the galaxy, they are known to be very territorial of the interior of these locations and will often viciously defend them from any incursion, human or otherwise.
