# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH5_Alien-Megacities\|Alien Megacities]]: Megacities In Your Campaign
This section establishes the foundation for the addition of megacities in your campaign. The **Default Assumptions** set the ground rules by defining the key traits of these wonders, whereas the Truth provides an option to make them fit your setting.

#missingArt _black triangle icons aligned right for each truth heading_

## Default Assumptions
The following are the default assumptions regarding megacities in the setting. These are generally true and are the key pillars for you to envision a galaxy with these wonders.

### Their People
**The cities' original inhabitants are long gone.**

The precursors—alien species that originally inhabited these sites—disappeared from the galaxy a long time ago.

### Their Condition
**The megalopoleis are in a state of disrepair.**

The structures are deteriorating and damaged, the infrastructure is rotting and corrupted, the technology tends to malfunction, and the monuments are decaying and weathered.

### Their Technology
**We don't understand the science behind these wonders of engineering.**

The technology of these metropolises has left us perplexed and amazed. We can only adapt or make superficial use of them and marvel at the results.

### Their Value
**Megacities are amongst the most valuable precursor vaults in the galaxy.**

In addition to offering advanced technology and a wealth of knowledge regarding precursor societies, these ancient settlements are highly valued as potential homes for mankind.

### Their Danger
**Megacities will never be entirely safe or secure homes for humanity.**

Despite adapting many of these vaults into fitting habitats, we will never be entirely safe within their confines. Their vastness and complexity allow hidden threats to emerge anytime, especially from the darkest corners and unexplored zones.

## Your Truth
The following is your truth regarding the control the precursor-made constructs exercise over the megacities. Choose one from the three options below or roll.

You may skip this decision and do it when it becomes necessary or relevant during play.

### Keepers Control
Keepers are constructs created by the precursors to maintain and guard their structures, settlements, and marvels. This truth determines how much control the keepers maintain over megacities and the likelihood of finding human habitats within them.

|  |  |
| :---: | --- |
| 1 - 33 | **Keepers have maintained near-total control of megacities.**<br><br>Their immeasurable numbers and territorial nature have ensured that most attempts to settle inside megacities have failed.<br><br>*There is only a small chance of finding megacities inhabited by humans* |
| 34 - 67 | **Keepers have partial control of megacities.**<br><br>While still a threat, their limited numbers have allowed humanity a modicum of success when settling inside megacities.<br><br>*It is unlikely to find megacities inhabited by humans.* |
| 68 - 100 | **Keepers have minor control of megacities.**<br><br>While megacities still harbor unknown dangers and challenges, the keepers' diminished numbers have granted humanity some success when settling inside them.<br><br>*There is a 50/50 chance of finding megacities inhabited by humans.* |
