# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH5_Alien-Megacities\|Alien Megacities]]: Exploring A Megacity
Even if humans inhabit a megacity, many districts, streets, roads, and corners remain unexplored. Conquering one of these sites is, in fact, a task that takes more than simply settling in one of its spaces. Ultimately, whether you are in search of knowledge, resources, technology, or a home, traversing a megacity is a rich, yet perilous experience. Here are the guidelines for implementing megacity exploration into your campaign.

## Investigating A Megacity
The main novelty of megacities is the unraveling of a mystery pertaining to the alien society that once inhabited these sites. For this purpose, your expedition progress represents not only the physical space you traverse, but the advancement of your precursor research.

However, bear in mind that the strange and alien nature of megacities makes their features and findings impossible to be entirely deciphered in a single expedition. The amount and depth of mysteries of these sites are as rich and inexhaustible as their original inhabitants.

To carry out an investigation within a megacity while undertaking an expedition, follow these guidelines.
- When you first make progress in an expedition within a megalopolis, choose a mystery from the *Megacity Mystery* table on page 173 or envision one based on whatever circumstance drew you to the site. The mystery can be one that naturally reveals itself during your exploration or a question that you have been determined to answer.
- As you continue to make progress in your expedition and reach new segments, use the *Megacity Cipher* table on page 173 for a revealing feature, object, or encounter that provides a clue to your investigation. To provide additional details, you may use the *Story Clue* oracle on the ***Starforged Reference Guide***, page 111, for a significant revelation, or the core oracles on page 12 for an abstract prompt. Here are some examples:
	- Action + Theme
	- Action + Focus
	- Descriptor + Theme
	- Descriptor + Focus
	- Action + Descriptor + Focus
- If the cipher is too complex to interpret, you may dedicate time to gaining a deeper understanding of the clue it provides, but do not get mired in the process. Instead, leave room for unanswered questions and consider how the cipher might reframe the mystery.
- When you finish the expedition, consider the questions brought about by the mystery and the clues you have uncovered up to this point. Then, propose a conclusion. The outcome of play reveals whether your assumptions are correct or if you have been misled.

## Reaching The Megacity Sanctum
When you explore a megacity, your objective is more likely to be found in the sanctum; the most important part of your journey. The sanctum does not necessarily represent a literal place, but rather indicates the pace at which you uncover a mystery, reach another milestone in your quest, or otherwise locate your objective within a site. As you venture deeper into the unexplored districts of a megacity, the hazardous nature of the site becomes more apparent.

You arrive at the sanctum when you...
- Mark seven progress in your expedition.
- Are prompted to do so by the "Transition into the sanctum" result in the Vault Feature oracle. When this happens, envision this shortcut and how it shifts the fate of your expedition.

## Using Oracle Arrays For Megacities
Megacities are alien constructions that are fundamentally **derelicts** but are deeply pervaded by a **theme** and experienced like **vaults**. Exploring them is an experience that makes use of ==**oracle arrays**==—combinations of multiple oracles.

The use of oracle arrays is explained in Chapter 5 of the ***Starforged*** rulebook on page 384.

When you are in an access zone leading to a megacity’s interior, do the following to prepare yourself for the use of the oracle arrays that can be found on page 174.
- Treat the megastructure as a **derelict** as indicated on page 350 of the ***Starforged*** rulebook. When navigating through the megacity, you will be prompted to roll for the derelict zone. For this, reference the Settlement column on page 351 of the ***Starforged*** rulebook or page 82 of the ***Starforged Reference Guide***.
- Choose a **theme** that best fits your vision of the megalopolis. You can find the *Themes* oracles on page 100 of the ***Starforged Reference Guide***.
- A megacity is a type of **precursor vault**, so you will also use those oracles as you delve into it. Precursor Vault oracles can be found on page 94 of the ***Starforged Reference Guide***.
- Additionally, you may be prompted to use the *Story Complication* oracle on page 57 of the ***Starforged Reference Guide***.

## Threats In Megacities
Spending extended periods in unexplored areas of alien ruins involves the risk of unseen dangers.

To represent the menaces of a ==**stalking threat**==, add a tension clock. When you suffer a cost through failure, delay, or inaction, you may advance the clock to show the threat drawing closer. The *Lurking Threat* table offers inspiration for these scenarios, including what signs you may encounter as the tension clock advances and what occurs when the clock fills.

Learn more about tension clocks on page 238 of the ***Starforged*** rulebook.

## Megacity Expedition Rank
The rank of your expedition into a megacity is chiefly determined by its size and complexity. Use the results of these oracles, on page 168, to reference the appropriate column and row on the table below.

It is important to keep in mind that other factors might also influence rank, such as the fictional circumstances and the importance of this endeavor in your story. Modify the rank of the expedition one rank lower or higher as appropriate.
