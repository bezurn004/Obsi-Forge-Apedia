# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH5_Alien-Megacities\|Alien Megacities]]: Megacities In Your Adventures
These marvels of forgotten ages might be used in your game sessions in many respects. Venturing into settlements originally inhabited by extinct alien societies offers a broad enough framework for countless premises. Powerful factions often sponsor expeditions to megacities in the hopes of obtaining significant precursor assets, but not all explorers are sent by patrons. Some go of their own accord. For what purpose?

Here are some reasons why explorers might interact with megacities in your campaign.

## To Know The Precursors
The mysteries surrounding the precursors' civilizations make their original settlements irresistible places to explore. There may be no better way to learn about their societies than to study and experience the places they call home.
## TO FIND A HOME
Some take it for granted that it is better to settle in what was previously home to other intelligent life forms. It is well known that conquering these sites is not easy, but given their pre-established nature and advanced technology, those who undertake this endeavor consider the inherent risk preferable to contending with nature and building a home from scratch.

## To Acquire Resources
We know that the precursors had an immeasurable capacity for creation, so it is not unreasonable to conclude that their cities may have served as storage for some of their resources. While we may not understand the properties of many of these materials, they will always be valuable.

## To Find Rarities
Many of the components of megacities are a mystery when it comes to their design and function. This has made them the subject of studies by interested parties. It is not mere curiosity that drives these interested parties to seek out these technologies, but rather a desire to obtain powerful artifacts for use or for trade.

## To Conquer
Many know the value of megacities and offer their assistance in taking possession of one of these wonders, reclaiming it for humanity.

Conquering a megacity is a challenge, but not a vain one. The ultimate reward of conquering one of these marvels is not only honor, but the pride in gaining knowledge, resources, technology, and a home for mankind.

Whatever the motive is for delver to venture into the megacities, this has proven to be the most enriching experiences to learn about the alien societies that once conquered the known galaxy.
