# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH9_Additional-Moves|Moves]] - [[ANWD_CH9_Failing-Forward|Failures]]: Learn From Your Failure
## Learn From Your Failure: Move Card
*Progress Move*

>[!abstract]  Trigger and Preparation
> **When you spend time reflecting on your hardships and missteps, and your failure track is +6 or greater...** ^trigger

> [!progress] Progress Roll
> Roll your challenge dice and compare to your progress.

> [!challenge-strong] On a strong hit
> You commit to a dramatic change.
> Mark one box on your quests legacy track, clear all progress on the failure track, and choose one.
> - Change course: Discard a single asset, take 2 experience for each marked ability, and **Advance** to spend this experience on new assets or upgrades.
> - Make it right: **Swear an Iron Vow** and reroll any dice.
> - Ready your next steps: Take +3 momentum. ^strong-hit

> [!challenge-weak] On a weak hit
> You learn from your mistakes.
> Mark two ticks on your quests legacy track and clear all progress on the failure track. ^weak-hit

> [!challenge-miss] On a miss
> You’ve learned the wrong lessons. Mark 1 tick on your quests legacy track and clear all progress on the failure track.
> Then, envision how you set off on an ill-fated path. ^miss
