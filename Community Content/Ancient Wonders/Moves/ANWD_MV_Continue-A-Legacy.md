# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH9_Additional-Moves|Moves]] - [[ANWD_CH9_Reworking-Your-Legacy|Rework Legacy]]: Continue A Legacy
## Continue A Legacy: Move Card
*Progress Move*

>[!abstract]  Trigger and Preparation
> **When you retire from your life as Ironsworn, or succumb to death or desolation,** you may create a new character in your established setting. ^trigger

> [!progress] Progress Roll
> If you do, roll the challenge dice and compare the results of the former character’s legacy tracks: quests, bonds, and discoveries (one roll per track). ^progress-roll

> [!challenge-strong] On a strong hit
> Choose one from below, or one from the weak hit or miss options.
> - Follow their path: Take one path or companion asset (regular or special) from the former character (at no cost), including any marked abilities.
> - Share a connection: Take one connection from the former character, including any accrued progress or bond benefits.
> - Accept an inheritance: Take the former character’s command vehicle and one module or support vehicle, as well as an additional item (at no cost), including any marked abilities. ^strong-hit

> [!challenge-weak] On a weak hit
> Choose one from below, or one from the miss options.
> - See it through: Choose one of the former character’s unfinished quests, and **[[SF_CH3_Swear an Iron Vow|Swear an Iron Vow]]** (with an automatic strong hit) to see it done. You may immediately mark up to half the former character's earned progress (round down) on this quest.
> - Rebuild a connection: Name one of the former character’s connections, and envision how time or circumstances have changed them in a dramatic way. When you **[[SF_CH3_Make a Connection|Make a Connection]]** with them, take an automatic strong hit and mark two ticks on your bonds legacy track.
> - Explore familiar ground: Name a location that was meaningful to the former character. When you first visit that place, envision how it has changed or is endangered. Then, mark two ticks on your discoveries legacy track. ^weak-hit

> [!challenge-miss] On a miss
> Choose one.
> - Deal with the aftermath: Envision how one of your former character’s foes has gained power or influence.
> - Switch loyalties: Envision how you begin in opposition to your former character’s beliefs, goals, or allegiances.
> - Open Pandora’s Box: Envision how an advancement or discovery has unleashed unexpectedly dire consequences. ^miss
