## Work Your Legacy: Move Card
>[!abstract]  Trigger and Preparation
> **When you leverage your downtime to develop your abilities, improve your resources, gain a reward, or boost your influence,** envision the challenges you overcome to acquire or improve assets for your campaign. ^trigger

> [!warning] Action Roll
> If you focus on…
> - Training for your quest: Roll +edge or +iron
> - Fostering your reputation for your bonds: Roll +heart or +shadow
> - Researching lore for your discoveries: Roll +wits ^action-roll

> [!challenge-strong] On a strong hit
> Mark one tick on the appropriate legacy track. On a strong hit with a match, mark two ticks instead. ^strong-hit

> [!challenge-weak] On a weak hit
> Mark one tick on the appropriate legacy track and fill a clock segment. ^weak-hit

> [!challenge-miss] On a miss
> Fill a clock segment. On a miss with a match, fill two segments instead. ^miss
