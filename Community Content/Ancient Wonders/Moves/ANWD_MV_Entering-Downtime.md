## Entering Downtime: Move Card
>[!abstract]  Trigger and Preparation
> **When you have at least one active campaign clock that threatens a vow (formidable or greater) and you spend extended time in a place safe enough to recuperate, profit, and develop your legacy** ^trigger

> [!into] Downtime Duration
> Determine the amount of time you are committing to the hiatus.
> - Half a season
> - A full season ^downtime-duration

> [!warning] Progress Track
> Then, as appropriate to that time, set the rank of the profit progress track and a number of segments for the downtime’s tension clock.
> - Half a season:
> 	- Profit Progress Track: Dangerous rank
> 	- Tension Clock: four segments
> - A full season:
> 	- Profit Progress Track: Formidable rank
> 	- Tension Clock: eight segments ^progress-track

> [!check] Downtime Ends
> When the downtime tension clock is full, you must **Depart to Quest**. ^downtime-ends
