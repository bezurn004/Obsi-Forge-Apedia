# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH9_Additional-Moves|Moves]] - [[ANWD_CH9_Making-Camp|Camp]]: Establish A Haven
## Establish A Have: Move Card
>[!abstract]  Trigger and Preparation
>**When you secure an area while on an expedition** after doing any of the following...
>- overcoming a critical obstacle
>- gaining meaningful insight
>- defeating a notable foe
>
>...envision how can you make this a perpetual safe place to rest. ^trigger

> [!warning] Action Roll
> Roll +wits ^action-roll

> [!challenge-strong] On a strong hit
> You make a reliable haven. Name it and mark the progress box in which this haven is being established.
> 
> Then, when you...
> - **Hunker Down** in this haven: Add +2 and choose an additional option on a hit
> - **Undertake an Expedition** from this haven: Add +1 and take +1 momentum on a hit.
> - Go to the haven by...
> 	- Returning to it in the middle of an expedition: Clear all progress after the haven
> 	- Starting a new expedition route: **Set a Course** and add +2 ^strong-hit

> [!challenge-weak] On a weak hit
> You make a reliable haven. Name it and mark the progress box in which this haven is being established.
> but making this haven causes a delay or setback. Choose one.
> - **Lose Momentum** (-3).
> - Clear one progress box and mark the haven appropriately.
> 
> Then, when you...
> - **Hunker Down** in this haven: Add +2 and choose an additional option on a hit
> - **Undertake an Expedition** from this haven: Add +1 and take +1 momentum on a hit.
> - Go to the haven by...
> 	- Returning to it in the middle of an expedition: Clear all progress after the haven
> 	- Starting a new expedition route: **Set a Course** and add +2 ^weak-hit

> [!challenge-miss] On a miss
> This place is cursed. You find no comfort in it. **Pay the Price** ^miss
