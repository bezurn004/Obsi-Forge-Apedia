# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH9_Additional-Moves|Moves]] - [[ANWD_CH9_Making-Camp|Camp]]: Hunker Down
## Hunker Down: Move Card
>[!abstract] Trigger and Preparation
> **When you rest and recover at an improvised, temporary, or make-shift refuge...** ^trigger

> [!warning] Action Roll
> Roll +supply ^action-roll

> [!challenge-strong] On a strong hit
> Your provisions are enough to adequately sustain you, your allies, and your special companions; each may choose one.
> - **Heal** and take an automatic strong hit.
> - **Hearten** and take an automatic strong hit.
> - **Repair** and take an automatic strong hit (in the field or underway). ^strong-hit

> [!challenge-weak] On a weak hit
> Your provisions are enough to adequately sustain you, your allies, and your special companions; each may choose one.
> - **Heal** and take an automatic strong hit.
> - **Hearten** and take an automatic strong hit.
> - **Repair** and take an automatic strong hit (in the field or underway).
>
> However, resources or time are wasted; **Sacrifice Resources** (-1) or **Lose Momentum** (-2). ^weak-hit

> [!challenge-miss] On a miss
> You take no respite. **Pay the Price** ^miss
