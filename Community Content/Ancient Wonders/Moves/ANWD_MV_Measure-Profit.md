# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH9_Additional-Moves|Moves]] - [[ANWD_CH9_Taking-A-Hiatus|Hiatus]]: Depart To Quest
## Depart To Quest: Move Card
*Progress Move*

>[!abstract]  Trigger and Preparation
> **When you take note of the fruits of your labor...** ^trigger

> [!progress] Progress Roll
> Roll the challenge dice and compare to your Profit progress. ^action-roll

> [!challenge-strong] On a strong hit
> Envision and record a ledger item with a wealth value per the hiatus duration and hit result.
> - Half a season: 3 value
> - A full season: 5 value 
> 
> **On a strong hit with a match**, record an additional ledger item with a value equivalent to a weak hit. ^strong-hit

> [!challenge-weak] On a weak hit
> Envision and record a ledger item with a wealth value per the hiatus duration and hit result.
> - Half a season: 2 value
> - A full season: 3 value ^weak-hit

> [!challenge-miss] On a miss
> The result of your labors do not translate into anything of value, and you become aware of some unanticipated complication or realization. **Pay the Price**. ^miss
