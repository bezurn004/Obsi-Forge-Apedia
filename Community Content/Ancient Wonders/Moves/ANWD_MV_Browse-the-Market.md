# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH9_Additional-Moves|Moves]] - [[ANWD_CH9_Exchanging-Wealth|Wealth]]: Browse the Market
## Browse the Market: Move Card
>[!abstract]  Trigger and Preparation
> **When you search in a community, physical or digital, to find an asset, service, or someone interested in what you offer...**  ^trigger

> [!warning] Action Roll
> Envision your approach and roll. If you...
> - Bargain across potential traders: Roll +heart
> - Assess the offers: Roll +wits
> 
> If you are performing the search in a dense and technologically advanced population, add +1.
> If you share a bond, add another +1.^action-roll

> [!challenge-strong] On a strong hit
> You find what you are looking for at a fair deal. If you...
> - **Resupply** now, add +2
> - **Exchange Wealth**, you have access to upmarket assets and services ^strong-hit

> [!challenge-weak] On a weak hit
> The search did not go as well as expected. If you...
> - **Resupply** now, add +1.
> - **Exchange Wealth**, you have access to upmarket assets and services but with some extra time; **Lose Momentum** (-1). ^weak-hit

> [!challenge-miss] On a miss
> You are out of luck; what you're looking for is not available and the situation worsens. **Pay the Price**. ^miss
