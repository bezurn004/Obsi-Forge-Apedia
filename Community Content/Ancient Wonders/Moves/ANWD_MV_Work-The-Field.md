## Work The Field: Move Card
>[!abstract]  Trigger and Preparation
> **When you use your downtime to profit or acquire something of worth,** envision the labor. ^trigger

> [!warning] Action Roll
> If your work mainly consists of…
> - Exerting physical grace or using your dexterity: Roll +edge
> - Working with people or using your charisma: Roll +heart
> - Exercising force or using your brawn: Roll +iron
> - Duping people or using your guile: Roll +shadow
> - Deciphering truths or using your intelligence: Roll +wits ^action-roll

> [!challenge-strong] On a strong hit
> Your work efficiency allows you to save for your adventures.
> - Add +1 on your next move (not a progress move).
> - Mark progress.
> 
> **On a strong hit with a match**, mark progress twice. ^strong-hit

> [!challenge-weak] On a weak hit
> Your work efficiency allows you to save for your adventures.
> - Add +1 on your next move (not a progress move).
> - Mark progress and fill a clock segment.
> 
> If you fill a profit progress track, you may set another and continue marking progress. ^weak-hit

> [!challenge-miss] On a miss
> You encounter setbacks during your routine and do not save; fill a clock segment. On a miss with a match, fill two segments instead. ^miss
