# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH4_Alien-Megastructures\|Alien Megastructures]]: Quarry World
> **Type 1 megastructure**
> A world consumed by structures spanning its surface that harvest resources.


Little evidence remains of what the surface of these worlds originally was like. It appears they were deemed to have only one suitable fate: consumption.

The resource requirements of any large civilization would eventually outstrip the ability of its homeworlds to supply itself. The logical conclusion is that worlds particularly rich in raw resources would be broken down and processed wholly to fuel the precursors' ever-growing needs.

## Original Biome
As you explore the surface of a quarry world, it is possible that you may find traces of the world it used to be before its consumption.

If you discover remnants of a quarry's world original environment, roll ==**>Planetary Class**== to envision these areas (reroll the "Anomalous World", "Jovian World", and "Shattered World" results; page 41).

## Oracle Quest

They report that following the discovery of a ==**>Descriptor**== + ==**Focus**== (pg 13), the megastructure's keepers started to act hyper-aggressively and began to ==**>Action**== + ==**Theme**== (pg 12). What are these individuals doing on the planet? Why did the keepers are more hostile than usual?_

## When Discovered

### Solar System Based
Roll ==***>Planetary Orbit***== (pg 22) to determine where the quarry world is located and mark it there. When you determine the number of planets in the solar system, reduce that roll result by one (to a minimum of one).

### Sector Based
Mark the quarry world at the center of the sector.
