# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH4_Alien-Megastructures\|Alien Megastructures]]: Ecumenopolis
> **Type 1 megastructure**
> A planet that once had a population so great, its got completely converted into a single, massive city.

Also known as shellworlds, eucumenopoleis is a megacity that grew to encompass the majority or entirety of their planetary body, covering their surface with a vast array of networked structures built across many segmented layers.

Given their scale, ecumenopoleis were presumably home to billions, potentially trillions, of inhabitants living between their layers. Due to their immense population, these worlds almost certainly relied on an interplanetary network of resources to sustain them. It stands to reason that when the end of the precursors finally came, all life forms on the planet died with them. However, the old saying has proven true time and time again: life finds a way.

## Exploring An Ecumenopolis
When you explore the surface of an ecumenopolis, you may treat it like any other world, generating the appropriate details with only the oracles present in this section. However, given that an ecumenopoleis is actually a massive megacity, you may use the content from Chapter 5 on page 168, which offers guidelines and oracles for exploring precursor settlements, learning about their species, and the threats you might encounter in these places.

## Original Biome
As you explore the surface of the ecumenopolis, you may find traces of the world it used to be. If you...
- Find that the ecumenopolis doesn’t cover the entirety of the planet's surface
- Discover remnants of its original environment

...roll ==**>Planetary Class**== to envision these areas (reroll the "Anomalous World", "Jovian World", "Karst World", "Shattered World", and "Tidally-locked World" results, page 41).

## Oracle Quest
_A ==**>Starship**== (RG, pg 58) in orbit of an ecumenopolis makes contact and requests your help in finding a group of missing delvers. They had advised the party to descend into the megastructure hours prior, and the team eventually reported finding ==***Descriptor***== + ==***Focus***== (pg 13). The delvers have not been heard from since. What did their group find within the ecumenopolis? What happened to the delvers?_

## When Discovered

### Solar System Based
Roll ==***>Planetary Orbit***== (pg 22) to determine where the ecumenopolis is located and mark it there. When you determine the number of planets in the solar system, reduce the roll result by one (to a minimum of one).

### Sector Based
Roll ==**>Coordinates**== (pg 19) to determine where the ecumenopolis is located and mark it there.
