# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH4_Alien-Megastructures\|Alien Megastructures]]: Megastructures In Your Compaign
In this exercise, you will learn the characteristics of megastructures to establish the foundation for their addition to your campaign. The Default Assumptions establish a baseline by defining key traits of these marvels, whereas the Truths are a set of options to make them fit your vision.

## DEFAULT ASSUMPTIONS
The following are the default facts regarding megastructures in a setting. These are generally true and are the key pillars used to envision a galaxy with these marvels.

#missingArt _black triangle icons for each of the default assumptions_
### THEIR BUILDERS
**The creators of these marvels are long gone.**

The civilizations that are referred to as precursors—the ones responsible for these ancient constructions—have not been present in the galaxy for an indefinitely long period. These alien societies, their disappearance, the purpose of their endeavors, the nature of their experimentations, and what they left behind are all clouded in mystery.

### THEIR KEEPERS
**These constructions are taken care of.**

Constructs left by the precursors are still, whether successful or not, maintaining these wonders. We call these constructs keepers. Despite the incommensurate amount of time without their creators, some sense of operability remains for many of these megastructures thanks to the efforts of the precursor-made machines.

### THEIR DEFENSES
**The keepers are overly territorial.**

While many megastructures may have external defenses, the keepers themselves are generally indifferent to mankind and creatures outside the vaults and megastructures that litter the galaxy. They will, however, often viciously defend the megastructures from any incursion within.

### THEIR MECHANISMS
**We don’t know the inner workings of these wonders.**

We can only assume their purpose and function based on their effect on the environment or the tasks they perform. Like the precursors themselves, their technologies have left us puzzled. Some argue that our capacity to understand these things is limited by our differences with the nature of their species.

### MANKIND’S CAUTION
**These megalithic vaults are the most hazardous in the galaxy.**

Due to their industrial and mysterious nature, precursor megastructures are considered to be the most dangerous places in the entire galaxy. While studious individuals strive to understand them, ordinary people fear the unknown of these structures and
will generally avoid them.

### MANKIND’S APPROACH
**Some undertake expeditions within the interiors of these vaults.**

Those who have the will to explore the depths of these structures are the ones we call delvers. Motivations for venturing into such dangerous constructions are many and varied. Whether the delvers want to conquer the structures, learn from them, or acquire exotic items, these explorers are renowned for being either the bravest expression of mankind or the foolhardy.

## YOUR TRUTHS
The following are your truths regarding key aspects of the megastructures: their size and rarity.

Choose a truth from the three options below or roll. If you are playing with others, give everyone at the table an opportunity to influence each one of these choices.

Don’t get bogged down in defining the details behind each truth. Use them as guidelines to inspire and surprise you as you play.

You may also skip this exercise and not define the truths upfront, choosing to define them only when it becomes necessary or relevant during play.

#missingArt _black triangle icons for each truth_
### TECHNOLOGICAL SCALE 
Similar to the Kardashev scale, the technological scale is a measurement system used to determine a civilization’s level of scientific advancement, focusing primarily on the scale of the megastructures a civilization is capable of building.

___

|  |  |
|:---:| --- |
| 1 - 33 | **The precursors were able to partially harvest the energy of planets.** |
|  | Precursors’ technology was just above humanity's. They could partially harvest the energy of worlds and build the first generation of megastructures, ones of sub-planetary scale.<br><br>_Only type 1 megastructures are discoverable._ |
| 34 - 67 | **The precursors were able to harvest the energy of entire planets.** |
|  | Precursors’ technology vastly exceeded the technological potential of mankind. Their constructions allowed them to harvest all the available energy of entire worlds, and they had the capacity to build planet-scale megastructures.<br><br>_Type 1 and 2 megastructures are discoverable._ |
| 68 - 100 | **The precursors were able to harvest the energy of stellar objects.** |
|  | Precursors’ technology completely eclipsed that of humanity. The prowess and reach of their civilization reached a point where they could harvest the energy of entire stars to allow the construction of the most awe-inspiring megastructures we have known.<br><br>_Type 1, 2, and 3 megastructures are discoverable._ |

### MEGASTRUCTURE PREVALENCE
Due to their technological sophistication, advanced materials, or size, the galaxy is not filled with megastructures. However, during our stay in the galaxy and expeditions, we have encountered many of them.
___

|  |  |
|:---:| --- |
| 1 - 33 | **Megastructures are rarely discovered in the galaxy we inhabit.** |
|  | The megastructures of the precursors are rare and sought-after sights. Few, if any, have been discovered in the Forge so far, though our greatest minds know with certainty that they exist.<br><br>_When you discover a new sector or solar system, **Ask the Oracle** (unlikely) if a megastructure is present._ |
| 34 - 67 | **Megastructures are uncommon sights to behold in the vast expanse of space.** |
|  | While still uncommon, some megastructures have been discovered in the Forge. Few of their numbers are active sites of scientific and engineering investigation. Others are being home to permanent human settlements, though their locations are usually closely guarded secrets.<br><br>_When you discover a new sector or solar system, **Ask the Oracle** (50/50) if a megastructure is present._ |
| 68 - 100 | **Megastructures greet explorers and travelers with regularity.** |
|  | Precursors' proclivity for megastructural construction seems to have known no bounds. Many of these wonders can be found strewn amongst the stars of the galaxy.<br><br>_When you discover a new sector or solar system, **Ask the Oracle** (likely) if a megastructure is present._ |
