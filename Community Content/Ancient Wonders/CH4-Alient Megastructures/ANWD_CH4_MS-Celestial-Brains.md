# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH4_Alien-Megastructures\|Alien Megastructures]]: Celestial Brains
> **Jupiter Brain — Type 2 megastructure** 
> **Matrioshka Brain — Type 3 megastructure**
> Incredibly large computers capable of processing possibilities and alternate realities.

Celestial brains are extraordinarily large data processor machines with immeasurable capabilities. Matrioshka brains entirely encompass a star, while Jupiter brains are as large as planets. Mankind can only imagine what calculations these unfathomable machines were originally built to process.

## Interacting With A Brain
There is no predetermined way to make use of a celestial brain. This technology is so alien and unknown that interacting with such a supercomputer can vary from case to case. However, per mankind's understanding, to access one of these supercomputer's interfaces you may have to...
- **Undertake an Expedition** to...
	- Reach its sanctum
	- Access a terminal inside its vast interior
- Establish remote communication from...
	- A console far from the megastructure
	- Your ship through some special technology

Once you have access to the brain's interface, determine how to use it through the narrative, either by accidental discovery, by undertaking a research mission, or through the **Gather Information** move (***Starforged Reference Guide***, page 10).

## Oracle: Sentience
Some brains may have gained a high level of sentience by design, error, or synthetic evolution. When interacting with one of these constructs, roll on the table below to determine its overall intelligence.

| :---: | :---: | --- |
| 1 - 10 | 1 - 20 | Non-sentient |
| 11 - 65 | 21 - 70 | Self-aware |

## Asking Questions
Use the guidelines below when you ask a Matrioshka or Jupiter brain questions. These will help you define the narrative boundaries and the question formats.

**Considerations**

1. Jupiter brains have less available computational power than Matrioshka brains. As such, they are only able to provide answers to questions relating to the sector or solar system in which they exist.
2. A computer will always provide correct answers that are free from errors. However, if the brain is damaged, dysfunctional, or only partially constructed, these answers have a chance to be discovered as not entirely precise later on in your campaign.
3. Some answers provided by the computer may not initially make sense given the information you have at hand. Take note of the information you have managed to uncover and implement this into the narrative at a suitable point.
4. The computer's disposition to answer is limited. When you ask the computer a question after the first one, **Ask the Oracle** (50/50) if it provides an answer. If no, it does not respond or refuses to answer subsequent questions. There may be countless reasons for this. It may be that the megastructure is sentient and its will prevents it answering more questions, that some component is dysfunctional or needs recharging before it continues. What will you do to determine the cause?

**Question Formats**

Most of the referenced oracles are found in the ***Starforged Reference Guide***. If you have any other oracle that you believe will provide a more suitable answer to the question, use that one instead.

- Who: ==***>Character***== / ==***>Faction***==
- What (abstraction): ==***>Action***== + ==***Theme***== / ==***>Action***== + ==***Focus***== (page 12–13)
- What (object): ==***>Descriptor***== + ==***Focus***== / ==***>Descriptor***== + ==***Theme***== (page 12–13)
- Where: ==***>Region***== (next page) + ==***Sector Name***== + ==***Space Sighting***== (page 27)
- When: ==***>When***== (next page)
- How / Why: ==***>Action***== + ==***Theme***== / ==***>Action***== + ==***Focus***== / ==***>Action***== + ==***Descriptor***== + ==***Focus***== (page 12–13)

## Oracle Quest
_Thanks to a recently completed study by xenoarcheologists of a ==**Descriptor**== + ==**Focus**== (pg 13), the scholars have learned that the finding was an integral part of a Jupiter brain located in a distant solar system. Following this discovery, a dangerous and recently declared rebellious ==**>Faction**== (RG, pg 72 or SI, pg 176) has made it their goal to seize this part, and with it try to corrupt the megastructure to further their interests. What does this faction seek to achieve? How do you plan to stop them?_

## When Discovered

### Solar System Based

**Matrioshka Brain** — Mark it at the position of the stellar object. The megastructure encapsulates the stellar object's majority or entirety.

**Jupiter Brain** — Roll ==***>Planetary Orbit***== (pg 22) to determine where the Jupiter brain is located and mark it there. When you determine the number of planets in the solar system, reduce the roll result by one (to a minimum of one).

### Sector Based

**Matrioshka Brain** — Mark it at the center of the sector map with the star that hosts it.

**Jupiter Brain** — Roll ==**>Coordinates**== (pg 19) to determine where the Jupiter brain is located and mark it there.
