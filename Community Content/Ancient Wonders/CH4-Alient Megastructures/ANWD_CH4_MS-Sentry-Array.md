# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH4_Alien-Megastructures\|Alien Megastructures]]: Sentry Array
> **Type 2 megastructure**
> A vast tower shrouded by exotic energies.

Very little is known for certain about this megastructure. While visible to the naked eye, this enormous tower actively thwarts any attempt to gain a comprehensive insight into its structure or mechanisms. Computers crash and fail, sensors return jargon and errors, and any camera fails to perceive the megastructure at all. The little understanding garnered regarding the purpose of this tower has been due to the delvers brave enough to explore them and fortuitous enough to return. Their reports speak of an interior that is akin to a labyrinth, disorienting and misdirecting any intruder, where every wall bares structures and machinery reminiscent of listening devices and data terminals. Stranger still every step is marked by disembodied voices and conversations. While we cannot be certain, it is through these fragments of information that we believe these megastructures functioned as part of a precursor spy network.

## Surveillance
While the method of surveillance can only be speculated, this megastructure has the ability to gather an incomprehensible amount of information from systems throughout the galaxy by monitoring, tapping into, and recording all forms of electronic communications. Individuals may wish to reach the sanctum of the sentry array to obtain information that may be critical to a personal endeavor, while factions may vie to control them to gain a strategic or diplomatic advantage over rivals and allies alike.

## Using A Sentry Array
The megastructure hosts vast arrays of monitoring apparatuses and unfathomably large archives of stored communications. When exploring the sanctum, you may utilize either. In such cases, these are the considerations regarding the intercepted communications.

### Was it relevant?

As the megastructure collects and archives a vast amount of electronic communications, you will not always be able to immediately obtain information specific to your needs. Instead you **Ask the Oracle** (small chance) to determine if the current zone of the sanctum offers relevant information related to what you are looking for. Increase the likelihood the more time you spend using the megastructure's systems or further navigating its depths, making the **Ask the Oracle** move each time it is appropriate to the circumstances. When you get a "yes", make the **Gather Information** move (***Starforged Reference Guide***, page 26) to determine the outcome of your findings. 

### When did it occur?

While you may use the megastructure to access any presently occurring communications, you may wish to access its vast archive of those that have occurred previously. When doing so, use the table below to determine how long ago these communications occurred.

| dice: 1d100 | Result |
| :---: | --- |
| 1 - 2 | Distant past |
| 3 - 10 | Years ago |
| 11 - 30 | Months ago |
| 31 - 55 | Weeks ago |
| 56 - 85 | Days ago |
| 86 - 100 | Hours ago |
^table-Communication-When

### What form did it take?

You may wish to determine what type of communication was intercepted and recorded by using the table below. After getting the result, **Ask the Oracle** (50/50) if the form of communication was made by one individual or shows more than one participant. These oracle results may be interpreted as one or multiple of the same type.

| dice: 1d100 | Result |
| :---: | --- |
| 1 - 25 | Text-based message or doc |
| 26 - 50 | Audio message or log |
| 51 - 75 | Video message or log |
| 76 - 100 | Image or recording |
^table-Communication-What

### Who was participating?

When defining the participants of the message, you may use the appropriate Character oracles.

**What was its content?**
You may define the content of the message by using any combination of oracles. Most of these are found in the***Starforged Reference Guide***.
- Who: ==***>Character***== / ==***>Faction***==
- What (abstraction): ==***>Action***== + ==***Theme***== / ==***>Action***== + ==***Focus***== (page 12—13)
- What (object): ==***Descriptor***== + ==***Focus***== / ==***>Descriptor***== + ==***Theme***== (page 12—13)
- When: ==***>When***== (page 113)
- Where: ==***>Region***== (page 133) + ==***Sector Name***== + ==***Space Sighting***== (page 27)
- How / Why: ==***>Action***== + ==***Theme***== / ==***>Action***== + ==***Focus***== / ==***>Action***== + ==***Descriptor***== + ==***Focus***== (page 12—13)
