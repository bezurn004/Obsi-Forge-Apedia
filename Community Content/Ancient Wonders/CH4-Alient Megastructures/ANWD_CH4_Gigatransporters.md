# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH4_Alien-Megastructures\|Alien Megastructures]]: Gigatransporters
Gigatransporters are the most common megastructures of the precursor civilizations. They served as a means of moving cargo, vehicles, and people across great distances between the surface and orbit of a planet. These constructions negated the need to build additional spaceports or design vehicles capable of both atmospheric and space flight, and while a journey in these megastructures may have taken hours or days, by creating a direct connection between the surface and space, they provided a more inexpensive and safe way to transition between locations.

## Importance
The significance of gigatransporters is not only their ability to provide a safe means of travel between locations. They can also provide a means of escaping a planet or other megastructure should things go awry. Additionally, these megastructures can contain vehicles, items, goods, and data terminals with information about their associated planet or greater megastructure they reside in.

## Presence
Many megastructures were constructed with gigatransporters attached to them to either create a direct connection between the greater structure and the planetary body it orbits or as a way to transport resources into or from space more efficiently.

Worlds that host a gigatransporter but no megastructure may indicate that they are home to megacities or other precursor vaults of great importance. 

## Technological Level
Compared to other megastructures, gigatransporters could be considered low-tech or obsolete solutions. It is believed that they offer a glimpse into earlier stages of their technological development. Though as their sciences advanced, these megastructures likely were abandoned, becoming relics of their past.

Although we cannot be certain as to how effective these structures were at satisfying the needs of these civilizations, we can be sure that—eventually—the precursors found better means of transportation. Presumably through more extensive and advanced vehicle production.

## Composition
Gigatransporters are composed of three elements:
- The station: A structure—usually at both ends of the transporter—where goods, vehicles, and passengers are loaded and unloaded.
- The vessel: A container or vehicle that carries the cargo between stations, or from one station into orbit.
- The link: A rail or tether that connects the vessel to the destination station.

## Locating Gigatransporters
Due of the role gigatransporters can play in your adventures, such as moving on or off a planetary surface, it is necessary to know when and how to locate them.

At any point while...
- approaching a world or megastrucutre
- being on the surface of a world
- within the access level of a megastructure

...you may **Secure an Advantage** (***Starforged Reference Guide***, page 10) or ***Ask the Oracle*** if you detect the presence of a gigatransporter. To do so, determine your position.

- Being in advantage implies attempting to locate a structure in an open space, with clear visibility, or with the appropriate gear (at least two conditions must be met)
- Being in disadvantage implies attempting to locate the structure in an interior location, with obscured visibility, or without the appropriate gear

Then, correlate the move result with the table below and set the rank to **Undertake an Expedition** appropriately (***Starforged Reference Guide***, page 14).

| Secure an Advantage<br>Result | ***Ask the Oracle***<br>Result | Rank in Disadvantage | Rank in Advantage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strong hit | "Yes" within half the margin to 100 | Dangerous | Troublesome |
| Weak hit | Yes | Formidable | Dangerous |
| Miss | No | -- | -- |

## Launch Loop
A launch loop is an artificial, ring-shaped structure situated around a world, just above its atmosphere. This gigatransporter can extend partway or fully around a planet or greater megastructure and is supported by colossal tethers. The interior ring of these marvels contains the vessel that travels along the rail from station to station or into space. Above or below the surface of a launch loop, there may be habitable structures that contains cloud cities or ceiling cities.

## Mass Catapult
A mass catapult consist of a vacuum tube that goes from the launch station to the exit. The tram launches the vessel—such as a spacecraft—into orbit at enormous speed, like a giant blow dart. Smaller versions of a mass catapult might have been used as weapons to hurl great projectiles at orbiting spacecrafts.

### Skyhook
A skyhook is a cable attached to an orbiting station that hangs towards the surface of a planet or greater megastructure. Payloads are brought to  the end of the hook by a suborbital launch vehicle that then passes the vessel to the hook before descending back to the world's surface. Then, the station rotates around its center of mass to fling the payload into deep space.

### Space Elevator
A space elevator consists of a colossal tether that extends from a planet or megastructure's surface that is attached to a counterweight such as a station or an asteroid. The vessel, called a climber, travels from the ground station to the orbital station and back again, delivering the cargo or passengers into orbit or to the world's surface.
