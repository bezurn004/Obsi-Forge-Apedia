# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH4_Alien-Megastructures\|Alien Megastructures]]: Cylindrical Habitats
> **O'Neill Cylinder — Type 1 megastructure**
> **Habitat Cluster — Type 2 or 3 megastructure**
> Compact megastations capable of housing millions of inhabitants.

Cylindrical habitats are colossal stations apparently built to house large numbers of inhabitants. These structures are the most common type of precursor space residence, presumably due to their low material cost compared to other megastructures.

Cylindrical habitats are referred to as ==**McKendree cylinders**==, found alone, orbiting worlds, or in deep space; and ==**O'Neill cylinders**==, grouped in geometric-shaped arrays called habitat clusters.

## Mckendree Cylinder
McKendree cylinders are colossal, residential space stations that rotate on their axis to produce artificial gravity via centrifugal force. Teams of delvers have reported looking directly up in an open field within the megastructure and seeing their comrades standing upside down, looking back at them.

The central axis of the megastructure, while devoid of gravity, harbors transport networks that connect the megastructure's enormous interior surface.

McKendree cylinders were seemingly designed to emulate planetary environments. When you explore one, roll ==**>Vital World**== (with no ash and volcanic biomes; page 80) to uncover its terrains and possible human habitation.

### Oracle: Diurnal Cycle
Use this table to identify how the McKendree cylinder gets its light and creates a diurnal cycle.

| dice: 1d100 | Result |
| :---: | --- |
| 1 - 75 | Structure-spanning windows |
| 76 - 95 | Lights built upon the surface |
| 96 - 100 | Hovering lights in the central axis |

## O'Neill Cylinder
O'Neill cylinders are scaled-down versions of the McKendree habitats. Given that these are small, residential stations, they are much easier for mankind to conquer.

The vast residential needs of the precursor civilizations make this variant of the megastructure unlikely to be found alone. Instead, these megastructures are mostly found in groups forming geometric arrays to compose larger habitat clusters.

## Habitat Cluster
Habitat clusters are space colonies consisting of arrays of O’Neill cylinders attached to form a larger structure. The ends of the cylinders are attached to others by joints called knuckles. Given that knuckles don't rotate, they are areas of zero gravity.

These arrays have advantages over standalone cylinders. The knuckles allow for transportation between stations without the need for spacecraft; the cylinders can be longer as the array helps stabilize them; and type 3 civilizations could use these arrays to surround a stellar object and leverage its energy.

### Oracle: Array Shape
Use this oracle to identify the shape of the array of cylinders that compose a habitat cluster. Knucklebones, pyramids, and cubes might be grouped with others. If the shape is determined to be any of these, roll ==***>Array Quantity***== to determine the amount of arrays in the habitat cluster. Then, for each additional array, roll on this table again and reroll the "Rungworld, "Sphere", and "Topopolis" results.

| dice: 1d100 | Result |
| :---: | --- |
| 1 - 30 | Knucklebone<br>Six-pointed star composed of six cylinders |
| 31 - 60 | Pyramid<br>Tetrahedron composed of six cylinders |
| 61 - 85 | Cube<br>Hexahedron composed of twelve cylinders |
| 86 - 95 | Rungworld<br>Ladder-like ring composed of hundreds of cylinders |
| 96 - 98 | Sphere<br>Geodesic polyhedron composed of thousands of cylinders |
| 99 - 100 | Topopolis<br>Looping ring or torus knot composed of countless cylinders |
^table-Array-Shape

## When Discovered

### Solar System Based
**McKendree Cylinder** or **Habitat Cluster** (**Knucklebone**, **Pyramid**, or **Cube**) — Roll ==***>Planetary Orbit***== (pg 22) to determine which orbit the megastructure resides in. When you determine the number of planets in the solar system, reduce the roll result by one (to a minimum of one). Then, **Ask the Oracle** (50/50) if the megastructure orbits a planet.

- If yes: Mark the megastructure in the orbit along with the world that hosts it.
- If no: The megastructure is in deep space. Mark the cylindrical habitat in the orbit. No planets might be placed there.

**Habitat Cluster (Rungworld, Sphere, or Topopolis)** — When you determine the number of planets in the solar system, reduce the roll result by one (to a minimum of one). Then, **Ask the Oracle** (small chance) if the megastructure surrounds the stellar object (the answer is no if the precursors were type 2 civilizations).

- If yes: Roll ==***>Planetary Orbit***== (pg 22) to determine which orbit the megastructure resides in and how it encircles the stellar object.
- If no: Roll ==***>Planetary Orbit***== (pg 22) to determine where the megastructure is located and mark it there, along with the world that hosts it.

### Sector Based
**McKendree Cylinder** or **Habitat Cluster** (**Knucklebone**, **Pyramid**, or **Cube**) — Mark the megastructure at the center of the sector. Then, **Ask the Oracle** (50/50) whether there is a planet at its location. If yes, mark the world at the same position as the cylindrical habitat. If no, the megastructure is in deep space.

**Habitat Cluster** (**Rungworld**, **Sphere**, or **Topopolis**) — **Ask the Oracle** (small chance) if the megastructure surrounds a stellar object (the answer is no if the precursors were type 2 civilizations).
- If yes: Mark it at the center of the sector around the stellar object that hosts it.
- If no: Roll ==**>Coordinates**== (pg 19) to determine where the megastructure is located and mark it there, along with the world that hosts it.

## Oracle Quest
A ==**>Settlement**== (RG, pg 54; SI, pg 186) residing within a conquered habitat cluster has requested aid in overcoming an ==**>Anomaly**== plague (pg 194) infesting one of the cylinders; a plague they believe originated from ==***Descriptor***== + ==***Focus***== (pg 13). Time is of the essence. The anomalous plague is spreading. How will you prevent catastrophe? How did this community settle here in the first place?
