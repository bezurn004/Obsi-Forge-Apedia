# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH4_Alien-Megastructures\|Alien Megastructures]]: Stellar Engine
> **Type 3 megastructure**
> A propeller of a star that travels through the cosmos on a nigh eternal voyage.

When humanity first entered the galaxy, we detected some stars which exhibited movements that belied our understanding of natural laws. After much investigation, we eventually uncovered the reality behind these unusual sights: the massive megastructures we call stellar engines.

While many threats to a planetary body can be deflected and avoided, threats to an entire solar system—such as supernovas, star collisions, and black holes—would leave humanity with no choice but to abandon their worlds or the entire system. Stellar engines were at least one of the precursors' answers to these issues: not to abandon the territory, but to simply move it out of harms way.

## Shkadov Thruster
An enormous parabola exists above the pole of the stellar object. Its mirror-like interior face reflects enormous amounts of solar radiation, and while some is refocused back into the star, the majority reflects around it, generating enough thrust passively to propel the solar system through the void.

## Caplan Thruster
This variant of the stellar engine functions through two parts: a Dyson swarm orbiting the star's equator and a vast planet-sized thruster below its pole. While functional, large beams of refocused light are directed at the star's pole directly above the thruster, super heating a small area and blowing millions of tons of matter into the solar wind.

These gases are drawn into the thruster, where it is believed they are used to fuel an enormous fusion reactor that generates the power needed by the enormous engine and generate a beam of energy which is directed into the star's pole, presumably to counter-balance its thrust and prevent itself from crashing into the stellar object's surface.

## Oracle Quest
_Two ==**>Faction**==s (RG, pg 72 or SI, pg 176) are vying for control of a solar system that is home to a stellar engine. One faction outwardly claims their reason to control the solar system and its megastructure is to ==**>Action**== + ==**Theme**== (pg 81) while the other has not publicly stated any motive. One of the factions is rumored to be behind the recent ==**>Settlement Trouble**== (**Starforged** rulebook, pg 126), using it as a way to somehow gain an advantage. Why do these groups wish to control the stellar engine? How will you help resolve the dispute?_

## When Discovered

### Solar System Based
First, **Ask the Oracle** (likely) if the stellar engine is a Shkadov thruster; if no, it is a Caplan thruster. Then, mark the megastructure at the location of the stellar object.

### Sector Based
First, **Ask the Oracle** (likely) if the stellar engine is a Shkadov thruster; if no, it is a Caplan thruster. Then, mark the stellar engine and its stellar object at the center of the sector.
