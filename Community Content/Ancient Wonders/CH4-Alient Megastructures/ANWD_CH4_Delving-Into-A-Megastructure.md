# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH4_Alien-Megastructures\|Alien Megastructures]]: Delving Into A Megastructure
When undertaking an expedition into the depths of a megastructure, first determine if you are actually delving into its interior. The rule of thumb is to differentiate a habitable surface from an interior or surface not meant to be habitable. Some of these ancient wonders have a habitable–or formerly inhabited–surface, such as ringworlds, ecumenopolis, or celestial disks. Others don’t have habitable surfaces, such as Dyson spheres or celestial brains.

When you make an expedition on the habitable surface of a megastructure, treat the exploration of the environments as stated in its corresponding section. When you delve into the interior of any megastructure or a surface not meant to be habitable, use the guidelines presented here.

## First Impressions
Once you have determined that you are inside of one of these wonders or have access to a new zone within it, roll on the *Megastructure Inner First Look* table on page 100 to generate the aspects of the zone that are the most obvious at first sight.

Additionally, you may roll for the *Megastructure Gravity* on page 100 to reveal either the gravity experienced inside the whole megastructure or in your current zone.

## Setting Progress Tracks
When you are ready to undertake an expedition into a megastructure, make an array of tracks as shown on the next page. Alternatively, you can use the array of tracks in the expedition worksheet that comes with the ***Ancient Wonders Playkit***.

- The minor progress tracks represent the expedition through a single level of the megastructure. You will mark progress here as indicated by the level rank.
- The major progress track represents the overall expedition through the site and the depth level you are currently in. Completely fill one box on the major track as soon as you score a hit when reaching your destination with the minor track. When the time comes for you to determine if you find your objective, roll on the major track.

When you **Finish an Expedition** (***Starforged Reference Guide***, page 14), score a miss, and choose to return to the expedition, roll both challenge dice, sum them up and clear that number of total minor progress boxes. Don't raise the expedition's rank.

## Reaching The Sanctum
When you embark on an expedition into a megastructure, your objective will most likely be found in the sanctum; the most important part of the megastructure. Here, the most perilous, corrupted, and strange nature of the site is most evident.

You arrive at the sanctum when you...
- Reach the 7th level of the megastructure (7th progress in the major progress track).
- Are prompted to do so by the "Transition into the sanctum" result in the *Vault Feature* oracle. When this happens, envision the shortcut and how it shifts the fate of your expedition. Then, position yourself in the 7th level by filling your major track up to that progress.

## Using Oracle Arrays For Megastructures
Megastructures are complex constructions with a similar composition to regular derelicts, heavily marked with a theme, and as enigmatically hazardous as vaults. Exploring these wonders is a rich experience that utilizes oracle arrays—combinations of multiple oracles—to generate the details of your expedition.

The use of oracle arrays is explained in Chapter 5 of the ***Starforged*** rulebook, page 384.

When you have access to a megastructure’s interior, do the following to prepare yourself for the use of the oracle arrays that can be found on page 101.
- Treat the megastructure as a derelict as indicated on page 350 of the **Starforged** rulebook. When navigating through the megastructure, you will be prompted to roll for the derelict zone. When this happens, use the table on page 100 as an extension of the *Derelict Zones* table on page 351 of the **Starforged** rulebook or page 82 of the ***Starforged Reference Guide***.
- Choose a theme that best fits the characteristics of the site. You can find the *Themes* oracle on page 100 of the ***Starforged Reference Guide***.
- A megastructure is fundamentally a precursor vault, so have those oracles on hand as you delve into it. *Precursor Vault* oracles can be found on page 94 of the ***Starforged Reference Guide***.
- Additionally, you can reference the *Story Complication* oracle on page 57 of the ***Starforged Reference Guide***. 

## Threats In Megastructures
The interior of ancient alien megastructures are the most hostile and perilous places in the galaxy. Elongated stays in these sites inevitably lead to doom.

To represent this, add a tension clock to track a lurking threat when indicated by the *Trouble in the Megastructure* oracle array. When you suffer a cost through failure, delay, or inaction, you may advance the clock to show the threat drawing closer. The *Lurking Threat* table on page 101 offers inspiration for these scenarios, including what signs you might encounter as the clock advances, and what might occur when it fills.

Learn more about tension clocks on page 238 of the ***Starforged*** rulebook.

## Encounters Rank
The level rank also serves as a general guideline for assigning ranks to encounters within a megastructure==s.== Use the table below for that purpose. Keep in mind that the fictional circumstances might influence the encounter rank. Adjust it one severity lower or higher if appropriate.

| ---------------- | ------------------------------ Level Rank ------------------------------ |
| :---: | :---: |

| Encounter Risk | Troublesome | Dangerous | Formidable |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Low | Troublesome encounter | Dangerous encounter | Formidable encounter |
| Medium | Dangerous encounter | Formidable encounter | Extreme encounter |
| High | Formidable encounter | Extreme encounter | Epic encounter |

> Delving into the unknown depths of megastructures is not for the faint of heart or low of means. Tales abound of those who ventured in alone, unprepared, or inexperienced, and never returned. The ones who insist on making these perilous expeditions are warned to go in with strong gear, ample supplies, and the company of strong allies or companions.
>
> Chapter 7: Special Companion Assets (page 198) and Chapter 8: Item Assets (page 208) offer new types of cards for ***Starforged*** that make delving into megastructures a more feasible endeavor. Additionally, Chapter 9: Additional Moves (page 222) offers a set of mechanics for ***Starforged*** that allow you to recover in the midst of an expedition and learn from your misses in the long-term.
