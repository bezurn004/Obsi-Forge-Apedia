# ARCHITECTURAL MARVELS
All civilizations have needs that must be met to ensure their continued survival and longevity—residential, scientific, industrial, and many others. Megastructures are one way these requirements could be satisfied in the most extreme possible sense.

Megastructures are large, artificial constructs, either consisting of a singular structure or many parts working in tandem with one another to achieve their purpose. They can take any number of forms; from vast towers or enormous rings to star-encapsulating spheres or disks. These ancient wonders are the darkest and most dangerous places for exploration in the entire galaxy. They are endless sources of adventures and narrative tension. Below are guidelines for implementing them in your sessions.
