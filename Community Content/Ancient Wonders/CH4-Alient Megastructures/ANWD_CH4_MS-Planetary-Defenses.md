# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH4_Alien-Megastructures\|Alien Megastructures]]: Planetary Defenses
> **Defense Nexus — Type 1 megastructure**
> **Aegisphere — Type 2 megastructure**
> Vast arrays of world-protecting emplacements.

Every civilization needs great constructions to offer protection. In mankind's exploration of the galaxy, we have discovered that the measures taken by the precursors to defend their worlds match their civilization's ambition.

The precursors had two types of planetary defenses: the ==**defense nexus**==—composed of numerous counter-offensive emplacements around a world, and the ==**aegisphere**==—a set of force fields that cover it.

## Defense Nexus
While many of humanity's worlds are defended by planetside weapon installations or orbital battle stations, they pale in comparison to the destructive potential of a defense nexus. Few have dared to venture near one of these megastructures, and those who have lived to tell of the encounter speak of capabilities that would send shivers down the spine of the most hardened captain. These megastructures contain weaponry that can easily challenge even the mightiest of humanity's vessels, hanger bays that appear capable of holding thousands of vessels or constructs, and armor that can withstand even the most catastrophic of blows.

No matter their form, all defense nexuses share a single goal: to prevent anyone and anything from reaching the world they were built to protect. This has generated intrigue among researchers, particularly the delvers, who see it as an opportunity to discover what was most treasured by the precursors.

## Aegisphere
From the earthen fortifications of humanity's prehistory to the energy shields of today, protective barriers are technologies that have endured throughout mankind's history. It is through the aegisphere, however, that we have witnessed possibly the most sophisticated and extreme expression of a defensive measure.

These megastructural spheres orbit a world and, when active, project a vast, resilient barrier around it, preventing any and all objects from passing its boundary. As such, factions vie to control these planets as regardless of their potential dangers, the aegisphere renders them veritable fortresses.

One characteristics of this megastructure is its lack of an operations center. Instead, it seems to consist of numerous separate installations. And while some aegispheres revolve around worlds harboring the recognizable signs of other precursor developments, others do not, begging the question whether the precursors were attempting to keep something out or something in.

## When Discovered

### Solar System Based

Roll ==**>Planetary Orbit**== (pg 22) to determine where the megastructure is located and mark it there, along with the defended site that hosts it. When you determine the number of planets in the solar system, reduce the roll result by one (to a minimum of one).
