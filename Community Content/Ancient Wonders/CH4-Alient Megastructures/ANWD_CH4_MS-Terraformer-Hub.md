# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH4_Alien-Megastructures\|Alien Megastructures]]: Terraformer Hub
> **Type 1 megastructure**
> Floating, megalithic facility that transforms a world's entire surface and all its aspects.

The precursors built many megastructures to alter planetary environments through a variety of means: restarting a planet's core, manipulating a world's atmosphere, or heating its surface by redirecting sunlight. However, none other than the terraformer hub can subjugate every aspect of a planet so completely.

This enormous floating dome roams the surface of the planet, altering its nature. Although its mechanisms cannot be completely understood, delvers who reach these enormous complexes report that through a combination of the megastructure's apparatuses and legions of keepers, these facilities can alter any aspects of a planet to the point of creating an entire different world.

### Megastructure Status - Partially constructed
When you roll ==**>Planet**== (page 40) to generate the features of the world that hosts the terraformer hub, reroll the "Anomalous World", "Jovian World', "Karst World", "Tidally-locked World", and "Shattered World" *Planetary Class* results.

### Megastructure Status - Operational
When you roll ==**>Planet**== (page 40) to generate the features of the world that hosts the terraformer hub, do not use the *Planetary Class* oracle. Instead, use the *Altered Planetary Class* oracle.

When you explore the sanctum of the megastructure, you may attempt to use its systems to rapidly alter a small region of the world. If you do, introduce the features of another planetary class.

### Megastructure Status - Non-functional or offline
The megastructure affects its host world in one of the following ways:<br>1—75 As operational, but roll ==***>Altered Planetary Class***== twice (next page). The second result is the world's original class.<br>76—100 As if the terraformer hub were partially constructed.

### Megastructure Status - Dysfunctional
As operational, but roll ==***>Altered Planetary Class***== twice (next page). The second result is the original class. Additionally, the planet has powerful earthquakes and violent weather events.

### Oracle: Megastructure Status - Destroyed
 The terraformer hub affects its world in one of the ways below and, additionally, the area surrounding the megastructure is a grim wreckage. When you explore this area, envision the features of a grave world (page 54)

### Transitioning To Another Class
While undertaking an expedition in a world with more than one planetary class, you may interpret the "Enter a new biome" *Surface Feature* oracle result as a transition to the environment of the next planetary class. If unsure, **Ask the Oracle**. The megastructure status and its effect on the world imply whether the transition is gradual or juxtaposed.

## Oracle Quest
_A ==**>Settlement**== (RG, pg 54 or SI, pg 164) on a world harboring an operational terraformer hub has been experiencing ==**>Settlement Trouble**== (RG, pg 54) following the people of the community having ==**>Action**== + ==**Theme**== (pg 12). The leaders of the settlement are looking for an experienced delver to access the megastructure and use its systems to alter their local landscape to alleviate the difficulties they are facing. What happened to cause this recent trouble? What else might need to be done to resolve this crisis?_

## When Discovered

### Solar System Based
Roll ==***>Planetary Orbit***== (pg 22) to determine where the terraformer hub is located and mark there, along with the world that hosts it.  When you determine the number of planets in the solar system, reduce the roll result by one (to a minimum of one).

### Sector Based
Roll ==**>Coordinates**== (pg 19) to determine where the terraformer hub is located and marked it there, along with world that hosts it.
