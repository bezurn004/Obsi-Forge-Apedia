# ARCHITECTURAL MARVELS
All civilizations have needs that must be met to ensure their continued survival and longevity—residential, scientific, industrial, and many others. Megastructures are one way these requirements could be satisfied in the most extreme possible sense.

Megastructures are large, artificial constructs, either consisting of a singular structure or many parts working in tandem with one another to achieve their purpose. They can take any number of forms; from vast towers or enormous rings to star-encapsulating spheres or disks. These ancient wonders are the darkest and most dangerous places for exploration in the entire galaxy. They are endless sources of adventures and narrative tension. Below are guidelines for implementing them in your sessions.

# MEGASTRUCTURES IN YOUR CAMPAIGN
In this exercise, you will learn the characteristics of megastructures to establish the foundation for their addition to your campaign. The Default Assumptions establish a baseline by defining key traits of these marvels, whereas the Truths are a set of options to make them fit your vision.

## DEFAULT ASSUMPTIONS
The following are the default facts regarding megastructures in a setting. These are generally true and are the key pillars used to envision a galaxy with these marvels.

#missingArt _black triangle icons for each of the default assumptions_
### THEIR BUILDERS
**The creators of these marvels are long gone.**

The civilizations that are referred to as precursors—the ones responsible for these ancient constructions—have not been present in the galaxy for an indefinitely long period. These alien societies, their disappearance, the purpose of their endeavors, the nature of their experimentations, and what they left behind are all clouded in mystery.

### THEIR KEEPERS
**These constructions are taken care of.**

Constructs left by the precursors are still, whether successful or not, maintaining these wonders. We call these constructs keepers. Despite the incommensurate amount of time without their creators, some sense of operability remains for many of these megastructures thanks to the efforts of the precursor-made machines.

### THEIR DEFENSES
**The keepers are overly territorial.**

While many megastructures may have external defenses, the keepers themselves are generally indifferent to mankind and creatures outside the vaults and megastructures that litter the galaxy. They will, however, often viciously defend the megastructures from any incursion within.

### THEIR MECHANISMS
**We don’t know the inner workings of these wonders.**

We can only assume their purpose and function based on their effect on the environment or the tasks they perform. Like the precursors themselves, their technologies have left us puzzled. Some argue that our capacity to understand these things is limited by our differences with the nature of their species.

### MANKIND’S CAUTION
**These megalithic vaults are the most hazardous in the galaxy.**

Due to their industrial and mysterious nature, precursor megastructures are considered to be the most dangerous places in the entire galaxy. While studious individuals strive to understand them, ordinary people fear the unknown of these structures and
will generally avoid them.

### MANKIND’S APPROACH
**Some undertake expeditions within the interiors of these vaults.**

Those who have the will to explore the depths of these structures are the ones we call delvers. Motivations for venturing into such dangerous constructions are many and varied. Whether the delvers want to conquer the structures, learn from them, or acquire exotic items, these explorers are renowned for being either the bravest expression of mankind or the foolhardy.

# MEGASTRUCTURES IN YOUR ADVENTURES
These ancient wonders might be used in your game sessions in many respects. Cosmic-scale structures with technology that far outpaces mankind’s provide a framework that's broad and solid enough for countless premises. These are some ways in which you might integrate megastructures into your campaign.

### TO SATISFY OUR WANDERLUST
Humanity has greatly evolved from simple and nomadic origins, and despite the countless ages that have passed—from our first steps on land and our first space flights—our desire to wander has never truly left us. As such, many individuals choose to explore to see all that space has to offer, to prove something to themselves, or even simply just for exploration’s sake.

## TO EXPERIENCE THE THRILL
If you are a daredevil who likes adventure for adventure’s sake, megastructures offer the most extreme scenarios to conquer. Voluntarily being exposed to the brink of death and experiencing the struggle for survival is enough to attract anyone looking for challenges.

The sense of urgency and self-improvement permeates the deepest motivations of many delvers. Whether they seek some external satisfaction, such as a payoff, or internal ones, such as reclaiming something for humanity, they hold nothing back to test themselves. Some have recognized that the adrenaline is the immediate reward, and the experience is the ultimate trophy.

## TO FIND ANSWERS
For the most curious, the enigma surrounding the megastructures makes them alluring places to explore and study. The thirst for knowledge is the driving force for those who seek to delve into the deepest sanctums. 

It is not materialistic desire that is the reward for those who undertake these expeditions, but the yearning to know the precursors through the wreckage they left behind: their creations, the remains of their projects, and the results of their experiments.

## TO PROCURE RESOURCES
The creation of these marvels involved an incredible amount of raw material, so much so that it likely involved harvesting—possibly completely—entire planets and asteroid fields. Because of this, many see these wonders as an inexhaustible source that could be harnessed to satisfy the current demands of humanity and beyond.

Many of the materials that could be found would be so rare that their findings may instigate significant technological advances for the human species, although their discovery would undoubtedly entail many conflicts of interest and warlike confrontations.

## TO FIND OBJECTS OF POWER
Anyone with real ambitions for power or wealth would be willing enough to venture into the most dangerous places in the known galaxy. For some who believe themselves capable enough, the interest of delving into the depths of massive alien constructions lies not in the search for knowledge or resources, but in the power that resides in the precursors’ artifacts; power that is not only coveted by those who recover them but also by those willing to pay large sums to obtain them.

## TO BE YOUR SETTING
Some people have made their communities on the surface of megastructures or even within them. After arduous expeditions, they find comfort in these immense antiquities, believing that the dangerous nature of these sites is the strongest rampart and that the hazardous conditions of surviving there are far safer than the hellish human intrigues and conflicts elsewhere.

On the other hand, some claim that these communities are the refuge of the mad, criminal, or worse, and that these settlements would rather live their lives at the most extreme and dangerous fringes of space than face persecution by human society.

## TO BEAUTIFY YOUR SETTING
Megastructures are constructions whose presence inspires awe and amazement due to their great scale and functional importance. The discovery of one becomes news that resonates among human settlements due to the potential impact and implications.

The mere sight of a megastructure is a reminder of how the universe is a canvas of infinite possibilities. Lifeforms once dwelt here who were so intelligent and capable that they were able to build these wonders that amaze us to this day.

Megastructures are varied both functionally and visually, and they might very well satisfy an aesthetic need to embellish the background of your adventures. When you generate a megastructure, the appropriate oracles will provide you with enough results for you to imagine these alien marvels in a variety of ways. Each megastructure entry offers an illustration to give you a basic idea, but these constructions are capable of defying any human aesthetic preconceptions. Don't let any visual aids limit your envisioning of these wonders.

# GIGATRANSPORTERS
Gigatransporters are the most common megastructures of the precursor civilizations. They served as a means of moving cargo, vehicles, and people across great distances between the surface and orbit of a planet. These constructions negated the need to build additional spaceports or design vehicles capable of both atmospheric and space flight, and while a journey in these megastructures may have taken hours or days, by creating a direct connection between the surface and space, they provided a more inexpensive and safe way to transition between locations.

## IMPORTANCE
The significance of gigatransporters is not only their ability to provide a safe means of travel between locations. They can also provide a means of escaping a planet or other megastructure should things go awry. Additionally, these megastructures can contain vehicles, items, goods, and data terminals with information about their associated planet or greater megastructure they reside in.

## PRESENCE
Many megastructures were constructed with gigatransporters attached to them to either create a direct connection between the greater structure and the planetary body it orbits or as a way to transport resources into or from space more efficiently.

Worlds that host a gigatransporter but no megastructure may indicate that they are home to megacities or other precursor vaults of great importance. 

## TECHNOLOGICAL LEVEL
Compared to other megastructures, gigatransporters could be considered low-tech or obsolete solutions. It is believed that they offer a glimpse into earlier stages of their technological development. Though as their sciences advanced, these megastructures likely were abandoned, becoming relics of their past.

Although we cannot be certain as to how effective these structures were at satisfying the needs of these civilizations, we can be sure that—eventually—the precursors found better means of transportation. Presumably through more extensive and advanced vehicle production.

## COMPOSITION
Gigatransporters are composed of three elements:
- The station: A structure—usually at both ends of the transporter—where goods, vehicles, and passengers are loaded and unloaded.
- The vessel: A container or vehicle that carries the cargo between stations, or from one station into orbit.
- The link: A rail or tether that connects the vessel to the destination station.

## LOCATING GIGATRANSPORTERS
Due of the role gigatransporters can play in your adventures, such as moving on or off a planetary surface, it is necessary to know when and how to locate them.

At any point while...
- approaching a world or megastrucutre
- being on the surface of a world
- within the access level of a megastructure

...you may **Secure an Advantage** (***Starforged Reference Guide***, page 10) or ***Ask the Oracle*** if you detect the presence of a gigatransporter. To do so, determine your position.

- Being in advantage implies attempting to locate a structure in an open space, with clear visibility, or with the appropriate gear (at least two conditions must be met)
- Being in disadvantage implies attempting to locate the structure in an interior location, with obscured visibility, or without the appropriate gear

## LAUNCH LOOP
A launch loop is an artificial, ring-shaped structure situated around a world, just above its atmosphere. This gigatransporter can extend partway or fully around a planet or greater megastructure and is supported by colossal tethers. The interior ring of these marvels contains the vessel that travels along the rail from station to station or into space. Above or below the surface of a launch loop, there may be habitable structures that contains cloud cities or ceiling cities.

## MASS CATAPULT
A mass catapult consist of a vacuum tube that goes from the launch station to the exit. The tram launches the vessel—such as a spacecraft—into orbit at enormous speed, like a giant blow dart. Smaller versions of a mass catapult might have been used as weapons to hurl great projectiles at orbiting spacecrafts.

*92 ANCIENT WONDERS*

### SKYHOOK
A skyhook is a cable attached to an orbiting station that hangs towards the surface of a planet or greater megastructure. Payloads are brought to  the end of the hook by a suborbital launch vehicle that then passes the vessel to the hook before descending back to the world's surface. Then, the station rotates around its center of mass to fling the payload into deep space.

### SPACE ELEVATOR
A space elevator consists of a colossal tether that extends from a planet or megastructure's surface that is attached to a counterweight such as a station or an asteroid. The vessel, called a climber, travels from the ground station to the orbital station and back again, delivering the cargo or passengers into orbit or to the world's surface.

# MEGASTRUCTURES
To generate a megastructure, first use the *Megastructure Class* oracle (next page). Next, if using a sector sheet, follow the **When Discovered** rule for that class of the megastructure in its dedicated section. Then, determine the condition of the wonder with the *Megastructure Status* oracle on page 96. Finally, go to the dedicated entry of the megastructure for further details.

To explore these wonders, use the **Delving Into a Megastructure** guideline on page 98. If you would like to investigate a precursor mystery inside of these alien wonders, use the **Investigating a Megacity** guideline from Chapter 5 on page 170.

Even though gigatransporters are considered megastructures, they are not listed on the *Megastructure Class* table. Instead, the guidelines to use them are present in their dedicated section on page 90.

Megastructures are usually referred to with a greater-than sign (>). They are not oracles, but this is your prompt to generate them using the Megastructure Status table and the rules on their corresponding entry.

## MEGASTRUCTURE SHAPE AND MATERIAL
Every megastructure has a predefined shape, and the majority of them are assumed to be made mostly out of metal. However, if you wish to add a twist to any of them, use the *Shape and Material* oracles on pages 102–103.

## POWER SOURCE
The ways in which megastructures are supplied with energy may vary. Assumedly, fusion power was used to build them, though we can never be certain about the methods the precursors used to make these wonders.

In the present, some may generate power through solar cells, geothermal energy, or fusion generators. Others may be delivered countless power cells by keepers as part of a vast interstellar transportation network. When using the *Space Sightings* oracle, you may use the "Starship" result as a prompt to envision these constructs.

## WARP GATE NETWORK
If you have previously determined that the precursors were civilizations of type 2 or 3 in technological scale, any sector containing a megastructure will also contain a warp gate. You can learn more about warp gates on page 162.

## ORACLE QUEST
Each megastructure offers an oracle quest–an adventure starter where you fill in the gaps with oracle-driven results. The sources of these oracles can be either from ***Starforged*** or ***Sundered Isles***.

## QUANTITY OF MEGASTRUCTURES
The default assumption for most megastructures is that only one may exist in any individual sector or solar system. This doesn't count for gigatransporters, which can be present without quantity restriction by inhabiting worlds or the greater megastructures enlisted in this section.

If you wish to include more megastructures in your setting, use the following guidelines. This assumes that your *Megastructure Prevalence* truth is that these marvels greet explorers and travelers with regularity. You determine your megastructure truths on page 87.

In a solar system confirmed to contain a megastructure, roll on the following table and reference the column that corresponds with your previously established truth about the precursor technological scale.

| Type 1 | Type 2 | Type 3 | Total Megastructures |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | --- |
| 1–75 | 1–50 | 1–25 | One |
| 76–95 | 51–85 | 26–75 | Two |
| 96–100 | 86–100 | 76–100 | Three |

After determining the total megastructures present in a sector or solar system, roll ==***>Megastructure Class***== (next page) to determine their types. When doing so, keep in mind that only one type 3 megastructure may exist in any individual solar system. If one is discovered, any subsequent rolls must be made referencing the Type 2 column.

Then, follow the **When Discovered** rule of each megastructure to determine its location. If they overlap or you find some conflict with the existence of others, you may reroll, change, or adjust the result as necessary. Below are some examples:

- After determining there are a total of three megastructures in a sector, you make your first *Megastructure Class* roll, resulting in an Alderson disk. Understanding that they span the entirety of the system, you forgo any subsequent rolls.
- When rolling for the megastructure class twice, you get the "Matrioshka Brain" and "Dyson Object" results. As both these megastructures would encapsulate the stellar object, you change the "Matrioshka Brain" result to a "Jupiter Brain".
- The location of an ecumenopolis and a kintsugi world overlap. As both would replace a natural planet, you reroll the last result, resulting in an equatorial shipyard that now encircles the ecumenopolis.
- Both an ecopoiesis array and quarry world result in the same planetary orbit. As their existence on the same world conflicts conceptually, you reroll the location of the ecopoiesis array, marking both it and its host planet at another location.

# DELVING INTO A MEGASTRUCTURE
When undertaking an expedition into the depths of a megastructure, first determine if you are actually delving into its interior. The rule of thumb is to differentiate a habitable surface from an interior or surface not meant to be habitable. Some of these ancient wonders have a habitable–or formerly inhabited–surface, such as ringworlds, ecumenopolis, or celestial disks. Others don’t have habitable surfaces, such as Dyson spheres or celestial brains.

When you make an expedition on the habitable surface of a megastructure, treat the exploration of the environments as stated in its corresponding section. When you delve into the interior of any megastructure or a surface not meant to be habitable, use the guidelines presented here.

## FIRST IMPRESSIONS
Once you have determined that you are inside of one of these wonders or have access to a new zone within it, roll on the *Megastructure Inner First Look* table on page 100 to generate the aspects of the zone that are the most obvious at first sight.

Additionally, you may roll for the *Megastructure Gravity* on page 100 to reveal either the gravity experienced inside the whole megastructure or in your current zone.

## SETTING PROGRESS TRACKS
When you are ready to undertake an expedition into a megastructure, make an array of tracks as shown on the next page. Alternatively, you can use the array of tracks in the expedition worksheet that comes with the ***Ancient Wonders Playkit***.

- The minor progress tracks represent the expedition through a single level of the megastructure. You will mark progress here as indicated by the level rank.
- The major progress track represents the overall expedition through the site and the depth level you are currently in. Completely fill one box on the major track as soon as you score a hit when reaching your destination with the minor track. When the time comes for you to determine if you find your objective, roll on the major track.

When you **Finish an Expedition** (***Starforged Reference Guide***, page 14), score a miss, and choose to return to the expedition, roll both challenge dice, sum them up and clear that number of total minor progress boxes. Don't raise the expedition's rank.

## REACHING THE SANCTUM
When you embark on an expedition into a megastructure, your objective will most likely be found in the sanctum; the most important part of the megastructure. Here, the most perilous, corrupted, and strange nature of the site is most evident.

You arrive at the sanctum when you...
- Reach the 7th level of the megastructure (7th progress in the major progress track).
- Are prompted to do so by the "Transition into the sanctum" result in the *Vault Feature* oracle. When this happens, envision the shortcut and how it shifts the fate of your expedition. Then, position yourself in the 7th level by filling your major track up to that progress.

## USING ORACLE ARRAYS FOR MEGASTRUCTURES
Megastructures are complex constructions with a similar composition to regular derelicts, heavily marked with a theme, and as enigmatically hazardous as vaults. The use of oracle arrays is explained in Chapter 5 of the ***Starforged*** rulebook, page 384.

When you have access to a megastructure’s interior, do the following to prepare yourself for the use of the oracle arrays that can be found on page 101.

When this happens, use the table on page 100 as an extension of the *Derelict Zones* table on page 351 of the **Starforged** rulebook or page 82 of the ***Starforged Reference Guide***.

Cylindrical habitats are colossal stations apparently built to house large numbers of inhabitants. These structures are the most common type of precursor space residence, presumably due to their low material cost compared to other megastructures.

Cylindrical habitats are referred to as ==**McKendree cylinders**==, found alone, orbiting worlds, or in deep space; and ==**O'Neill cylinders**==, grouped in geometric-shaped arrays called habitat clusters.

## MCKENDREE CYLINDER
McKendree cylinders are colossal, residential space stations that rotate on their axis to produce artificial gravity via centrifugal force. Teams of delvers have reported looking directly up in an open field within the megastructure and seeing their comrades standing upside down, looking back at them.

The central axis of the megastructure, while devoid of gravity, harbors transport networks that connect the megastructure's enormous interior surface.

McKendree cylinders were seemingly designed to emulate planetary environments. When you explore one, roll ==**>Vital World**== (with no ash and volcanic biomes; page 80) to uncover its terrains and possible human habitation.

## O'NEILL CYLINDER
O'Neill cylinders are scaled-down versions of the McKendree habitats. Given that these are small, residential stations, they are much easier for mankind to conquer.

*MEGASTRUCTURE*
# WARP GATE
> **Type 2 megastructure**
> An enormous generator of a portal to distant locations.

While space travel is a feat humanity can easily accomplish, the cost is time; time taken to recharge or refuel our drives at an anchorage, as well as the time that passes over the course of any journey. Warp gates are how we believe the precursors were able to avoid or mitigate this cost while also facilitating their logistical networks.

## TRAVELING THROUGH A WARP GATE
While the mechanisms of a warp gate are unknown or jealously guarded by those privy to their secrets, it is believed that with the appropriate knowledge, they can be used to travel instantly across vast distances.

### REQUIREMENTS
To activate and travel through a warp gate, three requirements must be met:
- **The warp gate you wish to travel through must be operational or dysfunctional.** While the status of any individual megastructure will undoubtedly vary, in order to use them for travel, they must be functional to some degree.
- **You must have the appropriate knowledge to activate the warp gate.** While there may be individuals willing to share this knowledge, the costs associated would be too great for most to bear. Instead, those brave enough may delve into a warp gate and, through exploration and study, learn the secret to utilizing these wonders.
- **You must know the location of another online warp gate to serve as your destination.** These megastructures seemingly were designed to function as a vast network, where a vessel may enter one and emerge from another. While the precursors may have been able to freely travel between any warp gates in the galaxy, the fact remains that we are unable to use them without first knowing the location of another one to serve as our destination.

### CONSIDERATIONS
When all requirements are met, you may choose to activate and travel through the warp gate network with the following considerations:
- **Operational:** If both megastructures are operational and you use them, you travel successfully with no narrative cost at your destination. If you are using the Starforged system, you may take an automatic strong hit when you Set a Course through the warp gate network to your destination.
- **Dysfunctional:** If one or both megastructures are dysfunctional and you use them, you travel successfully but face a cost at your destination. If you are using the ***Starforged*** system, you may take an automatic weak hit when you **Set a Course** through the warp gate network to your destination.
