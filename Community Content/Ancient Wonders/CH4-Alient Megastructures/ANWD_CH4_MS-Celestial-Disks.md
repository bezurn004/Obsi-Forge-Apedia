# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH4_Alien-Megastructures\|Alien Megastructures]]: Celestial Disks
> **Discworld — Type 1 megastructure**
> **Alderson Disk — Type 3 megastructure**
> Flat, rounded habitats that could house countless living beings.

These megastructures are enormous platters several kilometers thick which are designed to simulate natural environments. Their interiors contain technology capable of generating such great force of gravity to sustain life on their surface. There are two types of celestial disks: discworlds, which are planet-sized flat worlds, and Alderson disks which cover the entirety of a solar system and are known to be the largest megastructure ever discovered by mankind.

## Discworld
Mankind was not astonished so much as amused to discover that alien civilizations had constructed something that reflected our primitive understanding of the world.

Planetary in scale and flat in shape, discworlds were seemingly designed to emulate nature. When you explore the surface of one, roll ==**>Vital World**== (with no ash and volcanic biomes; page 80) to uncover its terrains and possible human habitation.

### Diurnal Cycle
**Ask the Oracle** (small chance) if the discworld features a diurnal cycle.
- If yes: The discworld is ringed by enormous engines that periodically rotate the megastructure, allowing both sides to receive light from the star.
- If no: The discworld rotates around the star in a fixed horizontal position, which causes the surface to exist in perpetual twilight.

## Alderson Disk
These unfathomably colossal megastructures are so large that, upon their discovery, they single-handedly shattered humanity’s understanding of material science. Encircling their host star, they stretch from the inner solar system to its very periphery, leaving no trace of the worlds and belts that once existed there.

Constructed from several individual pieces, each face of a segment was seemingly designed to simulate the surface of a world for an incalculable numbers of residents.

While the environments of an Alderson disk's surface may vary greatly when comparing individual segments, at the inner and outer edge of all Alderson disks stand walls many hundreds of miles tall. It is presumed that the purpose of these barriers is to contain the disk's atmosphere.

### Diurnal Cycle
**Ask the Oracle** if the Alderson disk features a diurnal cycle.
- If yes: The stellar object uses stellar engines (page 154) to shift up and down at the center of the Alderson disk, creating both day and night.
- If no: The stellar object is in a fixed position, which causes the surface of the Alderson disk to exist in perpetual twilight. 

### Alderson Disk Segments
An Alderson disk is constructed from five rings of six segments, totaling thirty individual sections. When you travel to a segment for the first time, roll ==***>Planetary Class***== (page 41) to uncover its environments. Reroll the following results:
- Chemical World
- Crystalline World
- Furnace World
- Jovian World
- Metallic World
- Karst World
- Shattered World
- Tidally-locked World

### Segments’ Edges And Atmosphere
**Ask the Oracle** (50/50) if the sections of the Alderson disk are solidly separated from each other in its inner edges.
- If yes: Immense walls segregate the atmospheres. Determine each one by rolling on the corresponding *Atmosphere* oracle of the segment’s planetary class (reroll the "None / thin" result).
- If no: The segments' environments transition naturally into each other. Use the *Atmosphere* oracle of the vital world (page 80) and maintain the result across all the segments of the megastructure.
- 
### Alderson Disk Gravitational Force
Due to the megastructure’s severe gravitational pull, any object without the ability to generate its own propulsion will quickly be pulled into the Alderson disk and potentially destroyed.

## Oracle Quest
*A small coalition of ==**>Faction**==s (RG, pg 72 or SI, pg 176) has requested an experienced explorer to travel to ==**>Sector Name**== (RG, pg 33) to collect data on a disk. They warn that all previous attempts have been thwarted by a previously unknown ==**>Fringe Group**== (pg 73). Why is the fringe group stopping the collection of this information? How do you envision performing research over such a vast surface?*

## When Discovered

### Solar System Based

**Discworld** — Roll ==***>Planetary Orbit***== (pg 22) to determine where the megastructure is located and mark it there. When you determine the number of planets in the solar system, reduce the roll result by one (to a minimum of one).

**Alderson Disk** — Use the sector chart with the Alderson disk map.

A solar system with an Alderson disk contains no planets or asteroid belts. Presumably, they were consumed during the initial stages of its construction or were pulled by the megastructure's severe gravitational force.

### Sector Based

**Discworld** — Roll ==**>Coordinates**== (pg 19) to determine where the megastructure is located and mark it there.

**Alderson Disk** — The entire sector map is the megastructure.
