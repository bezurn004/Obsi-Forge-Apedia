# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH4_Alien-Megastructures\|Alien Megastructures]]: Shipyards
> **Equatorial Shipyard — Type 1 megastructure**
> **Hyperstructural Shipyard — Type 2 megastructure**
> **Behemoth Assembler — Type 3 megastructure**
> Massive producers of spaceborne vessels and constructs.

Given the scale of the precursor civilizations, it is undeniable that they required vast numbers of space-faring ships and constructs to facilitate their needs. Automated and capable of producing an incredible number of units simultaneously, we believe these ring-like megastructures were the solution to that issue. These are three types of shipyards: equatorial shipyards, which encircle worlds; hyperstructural shipyards, which encircle stars; and behemoth assemblers, which are smaller than the other two, but designed to produce the largest units.

## Equatorial Shipyard
Encircling a host world, these enormous shipyards appear to be capable of producing vessels and constructs easily capable of rivaling mankind's mightiest creations.

### Oracle: Anchoring Method
Use this table to identify how the equatorial shipyard is kept in its position around the host world.

| dice: 1d100 | Result |
| :---: | --- |
| 1 - 30 | Colossal columns |
| 31 - 55 | Countless tension cables |
| 56 - 75 | Enormous thrusters |
| 76 - 90 | Powerful energy beams |
| 91 - 100 | Gravitational equilibrium |
^table-Anchoring-Method

## Hyperstructural Shipyard
Encircling a host star, these gargantuan shipyards are capable of producing ships and constructs so large that they would easily dwarf any of mankind's stations.

## Behemoth Assembler
These enormous shipyards, while smaller than their relatives, are believed to be the source of the largest precursor vessels. These megastructures are witnessed deploying vast fleets of constructs to the surface of the object it orbits.

### Oracle: Anchored Vessel

| dice: 1d100 | Result |
| :---: | --- |
| 1 - 10 | None |
| 21 - 40 | >Planet; pg 40 |
| 41 - 65 | >Planetcraft; pg 142 |
| 66 - 100 | >Precursor vault (world-spanning site, vessel); pg 92 |

## Shipyard Debris Field
Regardless of the current status of these immense shipyards, it remains possible that following their abandonment they continued to perform their function for a time. Newly constructed vessels would accumulate around the facility, forming an enormous debris field.

## Oracle: Debris Density
When approaching a equatorial or hyperstructural shipyard, use this oracle to determine the existence of a debris field and its density.

## When Discovered

### Solar System Based

**Equatorial Shipyard** — Roll ==***>Planetary Orbit***== (pg 22) to determine where the megastructure is located and mark it there, along with the world that hosts it. When you determine the number of planets in the solar system, reduce the roll result by one (to a minimum of one). 

**Hyperstructural Shipyard** — Mark it at the position of the stellar object that hosts it.

**Behemoth Assembler** — Roll ==***>Planetary Orbit***== (pg 22) to determine where the megastructure is located and mark it there, along with the ==***>Anchored Vessel***==. When you determine the number of planets in the solar system, reduce the roll result by one (to a minimum of one).

### Sector Based

**Equatorial Shipyard** — Roll ==**>Coordinates**== (pg 19) to determine where the megastructure is located and mark it there, along with the world that hosts it.

**Hyperstructural Shipyard** — Mark the megastructure at the center of the sector, along with the stellar object that hosts it.

**Behemoth Assembler** — Roll ==**>Coordinates**== (pg 19) to determine where the megastructure is located and mark it there, along with the ==***>Anchored Vessel***==.
