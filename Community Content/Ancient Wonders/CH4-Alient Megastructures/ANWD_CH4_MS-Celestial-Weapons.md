# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH4_Alien-Megastructures\|Alien Megastructures]]: Celestial Weapons
> **Nicoll-dyson Array — Type 2 megastructure**
> **Helio-obliterator — Type 3 megastructure**
> Networks of interconnected rings that form the greatest armaments ever discovered.

Many examples of the precursors' destructive potential exist within the galaxy, but few could conceive of the power of celestial weapons. Less knowledgeable explorers may initially mistake these megastructures for unusual or bizarre ringworlds, though their true nature becomes clear when considering their composition and energy emissions. They are weapons built to lay waste to entire planets or solar systems with a single shot.

Celestial weapons are gargantuan networks of rings bathed in heat and light that closely encircle their host star. There are two types of celestial weapons: ==**Nicoll-dyson arrays**==—which are made of independent Dyson rings—and ==**helio-obliterators**==—whose rings form an incomplete sphere.

It is generally understood that deploying one of these weapons against fellow human beings would constitute the gravest crime ever committed. These structures have inspired such awe and terror in all who witness them that it's horrifying to consider what sort of enemy would necessitate their construction.

## Nicoll-Dyson Array
These megastructures are formed by a network of independent Dyson rings. They collect the light of their host star, forming a tunnel to redirect it. The light is then focused and accelerated as a beam that travels towards its target.

## Firing A Nicoll-Dyson Array
If the megastructure is functional to some degree, upon reaching its sanctum you may attempt to fire its beam. The weapon is capable of utterly eradicating any target equal in size to a planet. If fired at anything larger, the beam will still severely damage or injure the target.

The beam of the Nicoll-dyson array will impact anything within its sector instantly. However, when fired at incredibly distant targets, the beam will take time to reach its destination.

## Helio-Obliterator
These megastructures consist of a network of rings that form an incomplete sphere that partially encapsulates its host star. The rings directly draw energy from the star, absorbing its matter in vast swirling vortexes. It is believed that, if fired, a helio-obliterator has the capacity to tear apart the fabric of space itself.

### FIRING A HELIO-OBLITERATOR
If the megastructure is functional to some degree, upon reaching its sanctum you may attempt to fire its beam. The weapon is capable of destroying any target equal in size to a star. If fired at a star directly, it will quickly cause the star to supernova. When fired, the helio-obliterator first generates a fiery energy storm with a wormhole at its heart that leads directly to the target. The beam then travels through the wormhole, instantly impacting its destination.
