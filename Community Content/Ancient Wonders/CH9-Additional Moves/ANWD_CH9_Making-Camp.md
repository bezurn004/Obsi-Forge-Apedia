# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH9_Additional-Moves|Additional Moves]]: Making Camp
Conducting expeditions into ancient alien structures is not a task for the average person, it is an endeavor for the self-sufficient adventurer, and no expert in the field can undertake such an enterprise without taking breaks in shelters.

Although it is possible to perform this action narratively, it is recommended for these breaks to be represented mechanically by making the following moves:

## Hunker Down
![[ANWD_MV_Hunker-Down#Hunker Down Move Card]]
___

Make this move to recover amid an isolated hazardous site. When doing so, roll +supply and resolve the result as indicated by the degree of success or failure.

**On a strong hit**, you, the other players, and your special companions (see Chapter 7 on page 198) choose to make one move from the list. **On a weak hit**, you additionally make a suffer move: **Sacrifice Resources** (-1) or **Lose Momentum** (-2). If you decide to **Heal** or **Repair**, you can also apply those benefits to your regular companions.

**On a miss**, you take no rest and the situation worsens. Envision what it is that prevents you from recovering, resting, or making camp, and make the appropriate move. Perhaps it is some inner struggle that haunts your dreams, the environment making it difficult for you to find proper shelter, or the sudden appearance of a hostile denizen disrupts your peace. If in doubt, roll on the **Pay the Price** table or **Ask the Oracle** for inspiration. Depending on what you envision, play to see what happens or continue on your expedition.

**Hunker Down** is not only useful when you are undertaking an expedition into megastructures. This move also provides additional use to the supply track by increasing its value within the game system. Delvers are usually forced to find provisions that allow them to survive and recover from their travels, journeys, and combats. Always keep an eye out for supplies. They will be needed in the most dire situations.

## Establish A Haven
![[ANWD_MV_Establish-A-Haven#Establish A Have Move Card]]
___

The **Establish a Haven** move offers a great benefit for long-term expeditions in sites of colossal scales. Although its use is restricted to a narrow narrative condition, it provides a stable location for the characters' survival. Alternatively, the VANGUARD deed asset allows you to establish a haven by other means and gain additional boons in these refuges.

When you reach one of the listed milestones, envision how that success leads the way for you to use that waypoint as a commemoration of a significant breakthrough in your quest. Such a place can be used to recover freely and safely and as a shortcut for future routes inside an expedition site.

If you **Hunker Down** in a haven, or make any recover move there, and score a miss, you may use the haven's well-being as the cost. Make a scene for it. What happens when you try to rest? What do you encounter? How is the haven jeopardized? What do you do to protect it? You may treat the haven as having an integrity of 5 that may **Withstand Damage** as an incidental vehicle, and you may Repair it as such.
