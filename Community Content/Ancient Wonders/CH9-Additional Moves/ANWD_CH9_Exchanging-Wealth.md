# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH9_Additional-Moves|Additional Moves]]: Exchanging Wealth
The moves presented in this section expand upon what is conceptually covered in the **Resupply** move by incorporating wealth as a currency.

The incorporation of wealth as a monetary resource in the ***Starforged*** system is explained on page 28 of ***Sundered Isles***. However, this is the necessary information for you to use this resource with only the content present in ***Ancient Wonders***:
- You can acquire wealth with the **Taking a Hiatus** set of moves on page 230 or by selling an item using the **Exchange Wealth** move on page 229.
- You record wealth as ledger items. A ledger item may have a wealth value of 1 to 5. These ledger items may be a stockpile of trade goods, payment in the form of credits, a cache of money, a relic, or a valuable item in general.
- Wealth is spent as a tool for narrative permission. However, it is usually not an excuse for skipping moves altogether, but to actually make them. For instance, with the **Exchange Wealth** move on page 229 you may hire a repair "average service" to make the **Repair** move "At a Facility", or you can hire a guidance "average service" to **Set a Course** instead of making the **Undertake an Expedition** move.

## Browse the Market
![[ANWD_MV_Browse-the-Market#Browse the Market Move Card]]
___

Use this move if you are unsure of finding a certain asset or service in a community right before performing the **Resupply** or **Exchange Wealth** moves for acquiring the desired object. Given that **Browse the Market** grants you an extra boon for the **Resupply** move, it is a safer option to leverage this roll first, especially if you are performing the search in a fitting community or if you share a bond with it. Take **Browse the Market** as **Secure an Advantage** and **Resupply** as **Face Danger**: a hit on the first move would allow you to get an upper hand when performing the second move.

**Browse the Market** also works the other way around: it might guide you in the right direction for someone interested in an object or resource you're looking to sell. The **Exchange Wealth** move allows you to trade mechanically with wealth resources.

If you are unprepared, **Browse the Market** is a good option to make sure you find the right place within a community to overcome an obstacle. If you are looking for a rare, costly service, item, or significance resource, the move provides you the mechanical means to justify looking for it.

When you make this move, avoid jumping into the next one. Instead, envision the hunt within the community. This is your best chance of fleshing out and detailing the populated place you are now in. What is your approach? What is this place like? How do the people react to you? Use some character and settlement oracles for inspirations. Take the roll outcomes into consideration. How did the search go, and why?

## Exchange Wealth
![[ANWD_MV_Exchange-Wealth#Exchange Wealth Move Card]]
___

**Exchange Wealth** is an alternative to the Resupply move for acquiring items in communities. Since there is no roll involved, this move allows for more secure acquisitions by using currency.

You may acquire something through this move that is as straightforward as a simple transaction for general upkeep (+supply). Other exchanges may have more nuances, such as hiring a service to **Repair** at a facility or at a anchor. Plausibility and Narrative Focus

When making this move, don't rush through it with a focus on the mechanical outcome. Instead, define the community well enough beforehand. This will provide enough background to justify what is plausible to find and what is not. Then, envision the circumstances. Ask yourself—or **Ask the Oracle**:
- Is it reasonable to find the service or object I am looking for in this community? If so, why?
- Is it possible to find someone interested and with the monetary capacity to purchase what I have to sell? If so, who is that?
- What is the trader or store that provides the service or item like? How does the interaction go and how is transaction carried out?

Some upmarket assets or services might require special narrative focus or implications. For instance:
- Objects of high rarity may be so valuable in certain communities that you may be perceived as an opulent individual. Will this work for you or against you? Will it bring short or long-term repercussions for your interactions with members of this settlement?
- Given their power and esoteric nature, abnormal objects may be banned in communities or have policies regulating them, so searching or trading for them may attract unwanted attention. When you ask about these items, how is your inquiry perceived? How do merchants keep the alien relics safe?
- Partnerships for your campaigns can be found in communities like any other service or item. However, the nature of these bonds requires more narrative focus. When you find a companion for your adventures, envision the scene. How do you bond with them? What makes them want to follow you aside from monetary interest?
- Costly services may require scene development. Training, for instance, implies the passing of time and invites the envisioning of a montage. How does it go? How does the world change as you dedicate time to develop new skills? If you have an active campaign clock, advance it.
