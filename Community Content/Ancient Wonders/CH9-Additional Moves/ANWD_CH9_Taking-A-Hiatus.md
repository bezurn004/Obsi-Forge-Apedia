# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH9_Additional-Moves|Additional Moves]]: Taking A Hiatus
A ==**Hiatus Stage**== is an optional structured approach for resolving an extended period of time. In it, your character is recuperating from actively undertaking the campaign while some latent threat is still developing in the background.

A hiatus stage is not a retirement from your adventures, but a preparation phase before the push. To start the hiatus stage, make the following move.

## Entering Downtime

![[ANWD_MV_Entering-Downtime#Entering Downtime Move Card]]
___

Start by identifying which campaign clocks represent forces that work against you. At least one of them should signify a menace to a vow of rank formidable, extreme, or epic. If you don’t have a campaign clock that represents an active threat in your story, you can’t make this move.

As part of this move, you set...
- A profit progress track with a dangerous or formidable rank. This represents the savings that you make as you work during your hiatus.
- A downtime tension clock with four or eight segments. This represents the time left before the hiatus ends.

Both the progress track rank and the tension clock segments counter each other and are determined by the hiatus duration. When making the **Enter Downtime** move, envision what out- of-control situation lead you to this point. Were you physically or mentally at rock bottom? Were you betrayed or facing a crisis of confidence? What forces you to recuperate, improve, and overcome the crisis?

Once the hiatus is underway, **Work Your Legacy** and **Work the Field** (page 232). You will not make any other moves during this stage. Consider this approach as a zoomed out perspective in which you will only use these two moves to resolve the scenarios that make up this time period.

The choice between the two moves is a narrative decision that provides you with different long-term bonuses.
- **Work Your Legacy** represents dedicating time to your character’s growth and evolution. With this move, you will mark progress on your legacy tracks.
- **Work the Field** signifies making efforts for material gain. It leverages the Wealth and Treasure guidelines from ***Sundered Isles*** on page 28, or the ones in the **Exchange Wealth** section of this chapter on page 229. With this move, you will increase the chances of having wealth in your ledger once you **Depart to Quest**.

The decision as to which move to make will always be based on your interest and not be restricted by any other condition. Whatever your call is, one thing must be kept in mind: the clock is ticking and the cost of the downtime is that the forces opposing you will also gain advantage.

As you envision your character taking these actions, be mindful about the commitment to the downtime. Avoid getting out of it and resolving a scene with any other move. You may detail moments spent in downtime as long as it doesn’t imply taking a course of action that would derail you from the hiatus stage. However, if you decide to terminate the downtime because of a narrative outcome, make the **Depart to Quest** move and proceed with the scene.

> You **Work Your Legacy** by researching the lore of a faction in an archive and score a miss. As a result, you envision the place being taken by a higher force. Because your character is in hiatus and not taking risks, you don’t **Compel** the gatekeeper or **Face Danger** to resolve the situation. Instead, you take this impediment to research as the cost to pay—as per the miss outcome—and continue with your downtime. However, if you wish to...
> - **Compel** or **Face Danger** now, **Depart to Quest** first.
> - Take actions later on regarding the entity that took the archive, you make a note of it for when you **Depart to Quest**.

**Hiatus With Allies**
If you are playing with allies, all players enter the hiatus stage at the same time. For this, all players must have a campaign clock that opposes the fulfillment of their vows or one that works against a shared vow.

Each player uses their own profit progress tracks and downtime tension clocks. Take turns to give everyone a chance to work their legacies or fields. Prioritize those whose tension clock has the least segments filled.

## Work Your Legacy
![[ANWD_MV_Work-Your-Legacy#Work Your Legacy Move Card]]
___

This is the move for character progression. Make it to mark progress on your legacy tracks. The translation of this exercise into experience allows you to acquire or upgrade assets.

When you perform this move, envision your character going through the process of bolstering their presence in or understanding of the galaxy. If they are committing to physical training, how are they achieving it? If they are fostering their reputation, how they are gaining renown? If they are researching, what discoveries are they making? Imagine the process as a montage of preparation and growth. On a miss, time has not been used as expected to make significant
advancement, and time is pressing.

If you are in the hiatus stage with allies and make this move, you may work together to envision all present characters in the montage. However, only the player making the move gets the mechanical outcome and the highlight of the envisioning. The rest of the players make their own move. The same principle applies to **Work the Field**.

## Work The Field
![[ANWD_MV_Work-The-Field#Work The Field Move Card]]
___

Make this move to gain wealth by leveraging one or multiple profit progress tracks when you make the **Measure Profit** progress move right after you **Depart to Quest**.

Wealth is the currency used in Chapter 7: Special Companion Assets (page 198), Chapter 8: Item Assets (page 208), and when making the **Exchange Wealth** move (page 229). To learn more about wealth, see page 28 of ***Sundered Isles***. Otherwise, follow the guidelines in the **Exchanging Wealth** section of this chapter on page 226.

When you perform this move, envision your character working on bolstering their treasury. On a hit, you make enough for the savings and you feel emboldened to continue with your hiatus. On a miss, saving becomes an arduous endeavor.

## Depart To Quest
![[ANWD_MV_Depart-to-Quest#Depart To Quest Move Card]]
___

When the tension clock is filled or your hiatus stage has been derailed, time is up. You must **Depart to Quest**. If you have made progress in one or multiple profit progress tracks by making the **Work the Field** move, make the **Measure Profit** progress move now.

Note that you don't **Depart to Quest** when a profit progress track is filled. Instead, as time is in your favor, you may continue making the **Work the Field** move to mark progress on another profit progress track. **Depart to Quest** when the downtime tension clock is filled or the narrative forces you to get back to the adventure.

As a consequence of going through a hiatus stage, you clear the slate for your character. Mechanically, you reset your misfortunes and battered impacts, as well as your condition meters. Narratively, your character is fresh and recommitted to their purpose.

Once downtime has finished, you are renewed and your vows call you back to your adventures, but your absence has given the threat that opposes you an opening. They have continued to push forward. What awaits you? How will you handle the new challenges? Who accompanies you in facing these new phases of the threats?

## Measure Profit
![[ANWD_MV_Measure-Profit#Depart To Quest Move Card]]
___

Make this move only if you have progress in a profit progress track through the Work the Field move. When doing so, make independent progress rolls per each progress track.

On a hit, envision the item you record in your ledger and how you save it, either as a part of your equipment or stored within your ship. This item might be "payment in credits", "stockpiles", or "goods". Each item acquired through this move is recorded independently and has its own wealth value. On a miss, a complication arises.

Wealth is the used in Chapter 7: Special Companion Assets (page 198), Chapter 8: Item Assets (page 208), and with the **Exchange Wealth** move (page 229). To learn more about this currency, see page 28 of ***Sundered Isles*** or the **Exchanging Wealth** section of this chapter on page 226.

Taking inventory of your savings is the last step at the end of your hiatus stage. Now, you are returning to the adventure better prepared with resources to meet the challenges that stand between you and the fulfillment of your vows. Hiring services, acquiring items, and maintaining your ship is a more feasible task. How else will you use your wealth? How will you go about securing it?
