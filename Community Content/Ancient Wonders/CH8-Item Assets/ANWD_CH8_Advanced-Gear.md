# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH8_Item-Assets|Item Assets]]: Advanced Gear
By default, ***Starforged*** does not focus on equipment. Your character's gear is abstracted in the supply value, and the objects you carry serve as narrative tools to influence your story. However...
- The **Check Your Gear** move (***Starforged*** rulebook, page 153) introduces particular items in your pack that you can use to overcome challenges.
- Some assets are object-centric, such as GUNSLINGER, which requires wielding a pistol, while other assets are vehicles or modules.
- ***Sundered Isles*** expands the concept of item management by introducing the treasury and wealth mechanic (page 28).

***Ancient Wonders*** expands the system by offering a larger item repertoire in the form of item assets. Just like regular assets, items are formatted as printable or preprinted cards.

## Item Asset Types
Besides vehicles and modules, ***Ancient Wonders*** adds the four types of items listed below.
- ==**Apparel**== must be worn to function.
- ==**Consumables**== are limited use items. If a consumable has been upgraded enough that it is no longer consumed on use, its type changes to one more appropriate.
- ==**Tools**== are gadgets made for a particular purpose. They are usually hand-held but may function as wearables.
- ==**Weapons**== are items designed to inflict harm or cause damage.

## Item Rarities
***Ancient Wonders*** outlines items into five rarities.
- **Common** [C]: Simple objects, such as food, water, fuel, regular weapons, ammo, and general upkeep. They are mundane pieces of equipment and provisions on hand and are implicit in the personal and hold supplies. These items do not have mechanical benefits, but instead satisfy narrative prerequisites.
- **Uncommon** [U]: Fine objects. This rarity and higher are characterized by either having a quality that stands out from the objects abstracted in the supply value or because they directly interact with the game mechanics.
- **Notable** [N]: Exceptional objects of above-average value. They are improved versions of uncommon objects.
- **Rare** [R] : Precious items. Support vehicles and modules belong to this rarity due to their cost.
- **Exotic** [E] : Wondrous objects of the highest value. By default, these objects are of alien origin, although some might be human-made according to exceptional narrative circumstances. Command vehicles, for instance, belong to this rarity.

## Precursor Items
By default, the rarity of precursor objects is handled with a "one tier higher" rule of thumb in comparison to human items.

| Precursor item | Human value |
| :---: | :---: |
| Common | Uncommon |
| Uncommon | Notable |
| Notable | Rare |
| Rare | Exotic |
