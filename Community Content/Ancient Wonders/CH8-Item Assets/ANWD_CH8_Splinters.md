# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH8_Item-Assets|Item Assets]]: Splinters
The most mysterious and powerful objects are not artifacts found in a glass case or on a pedestal, but at the core of the strangest, most alien locations. It was not until after numerous expeditions and failed encounters with anomalies that, after finally dispelling one, humanity came upon relics of powerful, esoteric energy.

==**Splinters**== are items from eons ago that have survived the crushing passage of time and are presumably the cause of the anomalies that emerged in the depths of alien sites. Given their properties, the goal of many explorers of ancient, alien ruins is to acquire these priceless objects of power.

Expert delvers to have obtaned splinters claim that their tiny size is deceiving. They bestow great power on their wearers, but only temporarily; they are prone to fading from existence once their energy has been harnessed. Additionally, splinters cannot exist for long without contact with some life form.

Some delvers remark on splinters' subjective effects as well. According to these explorers, there is a special thrill that comes with using these alien objects as well as a burning desire to gain their power. Everyone agrees that using a splinter feels as if one is closer to what they have come to understand as the physical and cognitive capabilities of the precursors, but they advise against carrying many of these items, as they become intolerable for reasons yet unknown.

## Acquiring Splinters
SPLINTERS are special assets in the form of blank cards. You don't include them in your item deck, nor do you acquire them with the **Advance** move. Instead, you acquire a splinter by successfully finishing an encounter by overcoming an aberration or entity, as explained on the **Anomalies** section of page 194. When overcome, whether by defeating an entity or directly seizing the splinter at the core of the aberration, these anomalies leave the item behind.
- An entity holds a minor splinter
- An aberration holds major splinter

When acquiring a splinter, use the oracles on pages 220-221 to reveal its properties. Mark each oracle result on a SPLINTER card. Then, name it. You may choose a name appropriate to the nature of the artifact or the anomaly that left it behind. For inspiration, you may use any combination of oracles, such as Descriptor + Theme (pages 12—13).

Alternatively, you can pregenerate splinters. ***Ancient Wonders*** comes with 12 SPLINTER blank cards for you fill and make a deck of them.

Finding splinters is one of the most addictive of pursuits for a delver. Explorers claim that the excitement of discovering new, unique, and esoteric artifacts is what drives most of their daring expeditions.

### Buying Splinters
Splinters may also be acquired through the **Exchange Wealth** move on page 229. When doing so, envision the nature of the trade. What is the merchant's reaction to your inquiry about such a rare artifact? How are they keeping the alien relic safe? What policies does the community have regarding these items?

## Using Splinters
When you generate a splinter with the oracles present on pages 220-221, you mark two key properties to use it.
- ==**Focus:**== This is the aspect of your character that the splinter affects when used. You may associate a focus with a stat, but not restrictively. Just like its *Shape* and *Material*, when determining the splinter's *Focus*, you may either roll on the table or choose one result appropriate to the anomaly the splinter came from.
- ==**Bonus:**== This is the mechanical advantage you get when using the splinter through a move. There are two types of bonuses—minor and major. If the item was dropped by an entity, use the *Minor Splinter Bonus* oracle; if the item was dropped by an aberration, use the *Major Splinter Bonus* oracle instead.

When you make a move using the focus, decide before rolling if you are leveraging the splinter’s power. Keep in mind that these objects are very volatile; when you use a splinter and roll a 6 on your action die, the item disappears after resolving the move. In this case, discard the asset. If you decide to use its power, make the move and apply its bonus. Envision the effect: how does the power of the relic manifest? How do the witnesses and environment react to it?

If a SPLINTER offers bonuses for different outcomes, you take all the bonuses that apply even after the outcome changes. For instance, if a splinter offers you a benefit on a miss and on a hit, and you score a miss, take the miss benefit. If that results in you improving your result to a hit, also take the hit benefit as well.

## The Splinters' Burden
Carrying more than two splinters bears an inexplicable burden. Some people experience it as bad luck, others as a spiritual malaise.

Having more than two SPLINTER assets counts as an amount of impacts equal to the amount of additional splinter. Carrying 3 splinters count as 1 impact (-1 max momentum), 4 splinters count as 2 impacts (-2 max momentum), and so on.

This burden is suffered individually, and not per the total of splinters carried by the party members, you and your allies.

Impacts marked this way cannot be cleared by anything but by discarding any additional SPLINTERS.
