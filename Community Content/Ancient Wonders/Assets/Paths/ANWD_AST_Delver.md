# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH9_Additional-Moves|Additional Moves]]: Delver Asset
Those individuals that dedicate their lives to exploring abandoned structures in pursuit of material gain have come to be known by the galaxy as delvers.

The DELVER asset that comes with this supplement offers bonuses while exploring derelicts and precursor vaults.

The first ability leverages the **Hunker Down** and **Establish a Haven** move, showcasing a delver's affinity for longevity in these environments.

The second ability represents the delver's ability to identify the value and practical use of the myriad of supplies or forgotten items they may encounter.

The third ability enhances their commitment to undertaking expeditions into perilous sites to search for high-value objects.

> ### PATH
> ## DELVER
> - [x] When you **Undertake an Expedition** or **Establish a Haven** in a ruin, add +1. When you **Hunker Down** in these locations, you may choose two options on a hit instead of one. If the ruin is alien, take +1 momentum on a hit when making these moves.
> - [ ] When you **Resupply** in an abandoned ship or structure and score a hit, you leverage your practical experience scavenging these locations; take +1 supply and +1 momentum. When you **Make a Discovery**, you capitalize on your findings; mark one box on your discoveries legacy track instead of marking 2 ticks.
> - [ ] When you **Swear an Iron Vow** to hunt for a valuable item or lost artifact, reroll any dice. If the sought-after object is not human-made, take an automatic strong hit. When you **Reach a Milestone** on this quest, take +1 momentum.
