> - [ ] Ability 2
> When the commando makes a move by shooting at a specific target, take +1 momentum on a hit. If they score a miss, the commando sets their sights on their quarry and marks it. The next time they make a move to shoot at the same target, you may reroll any dice and take +2 momentum on a hit.

## Commentary
These are the pillars of security in the different worlds of humanity; true soldiers with nerves of steel. Thanks to their tactical skills and exhaustive training, they are the executing arm of the statesmen's military strategies or the tireless defenders of the weak. They can range from the staunchest patriots to the most ruthless conquerors.
