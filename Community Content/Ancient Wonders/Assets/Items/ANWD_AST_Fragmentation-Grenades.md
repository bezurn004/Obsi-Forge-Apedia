> ### ITEM - WEAPON
> # FRAGMENTATION GRENADES
> Ordnances that disperse dangerous shards upon detonation.
> 
> | TRACK | 1 | 2 | 3 |
> | --- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
> | ORDINANCE | __ | __ | __ |
> - [ ] BROKEN
> - [ ] CONSUMED
^Framentation-Grenades-Core

> [!note] Uncommon Ability
> - [ ] When you make a move using a fragmentation grenade against an unarmored target or group of foes, add +2 and take +1 momentum on a hit. Then, suffer -1 ordnance.
^Framentation-Grenades-Uncommon

> [!help] Notable Ability
> - [ ] When you make a combat move using a fragmentation grenade, suffer -1 ordnance and choose one (decide after rolling).
> - Increased radius: Reroll one challenge die
> - Increased impact: Mark progress on a hit
^Framentation-Grenades-Notable

> [!check] Rare Ability
> - [ ] Take both outcomes above.
^Framentation-Grenades-Rare

> [!example] Exotic Ability
> - [ ] Two fragmentation grenades may be fused together and thrown to cause a significantly destructive blast. If you **Take Decisive Action** by doing so, suffer -2 ordnance. Then, you may reroll any dice, but count a weak hit as a miss.
^Framentation-Grenades-Exotic

## Commentary
While targeted weapons are permitted by most settlements, the ownership of explosives is restricted by many due to their inherent risks. There is no more cautionary tale than a grenade detonating during a brawl gone wrong, resulting in mass casualties.
