# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH8_Item-Assets|Item Assets]]: Shield Generator
> ### ITEM - TOOL
> ## SHIELD GENERATOR
> A device that creates an energy shield around the user.
> 
> | TRACK | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
> | --- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
> | SHIELD | __ | __ | __ | __ | __ | __ |
> - [ ] BROKEN
^Shield-Generator-Core

> [!note] Uncommon Ability
> - [ ] When you raise your shield, roll +edge or +wits. On a strong hit, set your shield to 5. On a weak hit, make it 3. Then, if you **Endure Harm**, suffer that harm as -shield and any remaining as -health. Raised shields last for a few minutes. If reduced to 0, they cannot be raised again for an hour or so.
^Shield-Generator-Uncommon

> [!help] Notable Ability
> - [ ] You may **React Under Fire** by letting your shield take the blow. If you do, roll +shield. Then, suffer -shield by the harm you would have suffered and ignore any remaining.
^Shield-Generator-Notable

> [!check] Rare Ability
> - [ ] When you raise your shield, you may reroll one challenge die. When you **Endure Harm** with it, add +1.
^Shield-Generator-Rare

> [!example] Exotic Ability
> - [ ] When you **Endure Harm**, ignore harm up to the value of your shield and suffer -1 shield.
^Shield-Generator-Exotic

## Commentary
The miniaturization of the technology used by starship shield generators was a feat many generations in the making. Now, anyone whose life or work brings them close to danger seeks to own one, despite the maintenance needed to sustain their mechanisms.
