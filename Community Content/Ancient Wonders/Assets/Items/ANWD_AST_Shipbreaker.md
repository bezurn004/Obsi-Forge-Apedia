# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH8_Item-Assets|Item Assets]]: Shipbreaker
> ### ITEM - TOOL
> ## SHIPBREAKER
> An industrial tool to salvage vessels and break through thick barriers.
> - [ ] BROKEN
^Shipbreaker-Core

> [!note] Uncommon Ability
> - [ ] When you make a move using the shipbreaker to cut through a barrier (such as a ship’s hull, a vehicle’s armor, or a wall), add +1. Then, you may **Lose Momentum** (-2) to reroll one challenge die.
^Shipbreaker-Uncommon

> [!help] Notable Ability
> - [ ] The shipbreaker features a turbo. When you make a move using this tool, you may leverage its turbo after rolling. If you do, improve a miss to a weak hit, or a weak hit to a strong hit, but Endure Harm (-1).
^Shipbreaker-Notable

> [!check] Rare Ability
> - [ ] The shipbreaker may detect valuable materials. When you **Explore a Waypoint** or **Resupply** using this tool and score a strong hit with a 6 on your action die, you find a highly valuable resource; take +1 momentum. Then, you may choose to continue to look for more. If you do, reroll your action die and one challenge die. Resolve the move as a new outcome.
^Shipbreaker-Rare

## Commentary
Once, salvaging a vessel was a laborious process. To reduce their workload, a group of astute dockworkers designed and built the first shipbreaker. Although it achieved its purpose, their employer opted to reward their ingenuity by filling their schedules with more work.
