> [!note] Uncommon Ability
> - [ ] When you make a move using an implosion grenade against a light target or group of foes, add +1 and take +2 momentum on a hit. Then, suffer -1 ordnance.
^Implosion-Grenades-Uncommon

> [!help] Notable Ability
> - [ ] When you make a combat move using an implosion grenade, suffer -1 ordnance and choose one (decide after rolling).
> - Increased radius: Reroll one challenge die
> - Increased pull: Take +3 momentum
^Implosion-Grenades-Notable

> [!check] Rare Ability
> - [ ] Take both outcomes above.
^Implosion-Grenades-Rare

> [!example] Exotic Ability
> - [ ] Two implosion grenades may be fused together and thrown to create a short-lived, miniature black hole. If you **Take Decisive Action** by doing so, suffer -2 ordnances, take an automatic weak hit, roll twice on the move's table, and choose one result.
^Implosion-Grenades-Exotic

## Commentary
Developed by a facility investigating potential alternatives to gunpowder, it is rumored that the first detonation of an implosion grenade resulted in the devastation of a laboratory. Despite the great risks, mass production quickly came into effect to demonstrating human foolishness.
