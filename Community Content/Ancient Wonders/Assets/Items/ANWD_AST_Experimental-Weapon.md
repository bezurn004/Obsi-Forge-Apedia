# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH8_Item-Assets|Item Assets]]: Experimental Weapon
> ### ITEM - WEAPON
> ## EXPERIMENTAL WEAPON
> A weapon infused with elemental energies.
> 
> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | WEAPON TYPE |  |
> - [ ] BROKEN
^Experimental-Weapon-Core

> [!note] Uncommon Ability
> - [ ] When you make a combat move using the weapon, add +1 and take +1 momentum on a hit. When you score a miss as a result, **Pay the Price** by choosing one.
> - Volatile: **Endure Harm** (1 harm)
> - Fragile: Mark the weapon as broken
^Experimental-Weapon-Uncommon

> [!help] Notable Ability
> - [ ] When you make a move by using the weapon, you may reroll one challenge die.
^Experimental-Weapon-Notable

> [!check] Rare Ability
> - [ ] When you score a miss on a combat move with the weapon, **Pay the Price** as normal.
^Experimental-Weapon-Rare

> [!example] Exotic Ability
> - [ ] When you make a combat move using the weapon, you may **Lose Momentum** (-3) to choose one (after rolling).
> - Overcharged: Mark progress on a hit 
> - Focused: Improve a miss to a weak hit, or a weak hit to a strong hit.
^Experimental-Weapon-Rare

## Commentary
Humanity has a variety of arms at its disposal that function within normal parameters. There are some weapons, however, that break these norms and instead use the universe's diverse energies to fulfill their purpose. Their rarity makes those wielding them particular dangerous in battle.
