# SPECIALIZED PARTNERSHIP
Among the delvers, there are several rules about the exploration of megastructures. The most important is to never go alone. A partnership with at least one specialized companion or capable
ally is required.

==**Special companions**== are a new type of asset. These companions are stronger than regular ones. The are designed to cover the mechanical absence of allies while providing an advantageous resource for undertaking expeditions in the most dangerous locations.

Even though they specialize in delving into megastructures, they can also be your loyal companions on regular missions.

# COMPOSITION OF SPECIAL COMPANIONS
Special companions are composed of two cards instead of one, and they feature...
- A stats array
- A health meter
- A spirit meter
- Three abilities
- One master ability

## STATS ARRAY
Even though each special companion features a default stat array, you can always tweak it to fit the needs of your campaign or your particular vision of your special companion.

## SPECIAL ABILITIES
One of the defining characteristics of special companions is their abilities.

The three abilities featured on the second card are proportionally more powerful than those of regular companion assets, which is why the cost of acquiring and upgrading special companions is higher.

The master ability surpasses the other abilities in mechanical advantages. Its acquisition is not dependent on experience, but rather the fulfillment of a vow which revolves around the special companion.

These features of special companions are detailed on the next page.

# USING SPECIAL COMPANIONS
Unlike regular companions, special companions have their own stats and spirit meter. This means that they have their own capabilities and can make their own moves. These are the caveats:
- They use the asset owner's momentum meter and legacy tracks.
- The term "ally" applies to them for the **Sojourn** and **Aid Your Ally** moves. They don't make the **Sojourn** move themselves.
- When you **Heal** from them, you cannot choose to "Receive treatment from someone". Instead, they make the **Heal** move with the "Provide care" option.
- When you **Repair** from them, you cannot choose to "Obtain repair from someone". Instead, they make the **Repair** move with the "Make your own repairs" option.
- They don't use the **Companion Takes a Hit** move, but they **Endure Harm** and **Endure Stress** instead.
- They cannot have burdens (doomed and tormented impacts).
- They don't make quest, connection, legacy, and progress moves. When these moves are prompted, they are assumed to be made by the asset owner.

Aside from these exceptions, special companions play the same as regular ones. If you ever have a question regarding the use of special companions, ask yourself: "How would this be handled if it were a regular companion?"

## SPECIAL COMPANIONS SUFFERING LASTING EFFECTS
When a special companion is marked with a lasting effect impact, you don't reduce your max momentum on your momentum reset. Instead, you strike or cover the value 5 on your special companion meter appropriately...
- If they get ==**permanently harmed**==, strike or cover the 5 on their health meter.
- If they get ==**traumatized**==, strike or cover the 5 on their spirit meter.

This means that their max value of the marked meter has been reduced by one. As a result, envision the nature of this impact on the special companion, physically, emotionally, and mentally. How do they interact with the world? How does this impact affect their outlook, methods, or their relationships with others?

## SPECIAL COMPANIONS AND OTHER ASSETS
While special companions cannot acquire their own assets, they can use assets that are physical objects, including items and vehicles. Once acquired, the owner of these assets can allocate them to a special companion.

# HIRING AND UPGRADING
You acquire special companions through the ***Ancient Wonders*** **Advance** move (4 experience to obtain and 3 to upgrade) and the **Exchange Wealth** move (5 wealth to obtain and 4 to upgrade). When you acquire a special companion, do the following:
- Give them a name.
- Envision their look and personality.
- Set the condition meters to their max value.
- Choose one regular starting ability from the three listed on the abilities card.
- Write the special companion mastery vow, mark it, and give it a rank of formidable or extreme.
- Set a scene with a narrative focus. How do you bond with the special companion? If you acquire them with wealth, what makes them want to follow you aside from monetary interest?

## SPECIAL COMPANIONS MASTERY VOW
Each special companion listed in this section has a mastery quest starter. This represents a lingering ambition or unrelenting desire of the companions. When you acquire a special companion, make their quest starter a concrete mastery vow. By default, these quests are of formidable rank, but you can make them extreme if you wish to make them more prominent or difficult in your story.

The special companion mastery vow is linked to their master ability. Once you have ended this quest successfully, you unlock the ability on the card with the stats and condition meters.

The mastery vow is not a prerequisite to acquiring special companion assets. Instead, it is an opportunity for special companions to grow and acquire permanent, special capabilities for future missions in your campaign. The fulfillment of this vow represents not only an improvement in their skills but also a stronger bond of loyalty to you.
