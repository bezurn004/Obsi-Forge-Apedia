# [[_Ancient Wonders\|Ancient Wonders]] - [[_ANWD_CH7_Special-Companions|Special Companions Assets]]: Composition Of Special Companions
Special companions are composed of two cards instead of one, and they feature...
- A stats array
- A health meter
- A spirit meter
- Three abilities
- One master ability

## Stats Array
Even though each special companion features a default stat array, you can always tweak it to fit the needs of your campaign or your particular vision of your special companion.

## Special Abilities
One of the defining characteristics of special companions is their abilities.

The three abilities featured on the second card are proportionally more powerful than those of regular companion assets, which is why the cost of acquiring and upgrading special companions is higher.

The master ability surpasses the other abilities in mechanical advantages. Its acquisition is not dependent on experience, but rather the fulfillment of a vow which revolves around the special companion.
