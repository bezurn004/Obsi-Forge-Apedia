# [[_Forge Horizons|Forge Horizons]]: Episode Summary

# EPISODIC ADVENTURES
The episodic adventure rules break down your Starforged game into manageable episodes where you discover the setting of your game through play. You don’t define everything up front with truths, starting sectors, or connections. These rules are ideal for solo play, or for one-shots when playing Starforged with a guide or as a co- operative experience.

When playing using the episodic adventures rules, skip creating truths, a starting sector, and a connection. Create your character with assets, stats, items, and a starship as normal. Also skip making a background vow. After character creation, your first session and each subsequent session of episodic adventures has three steps: 

| | |
| --- | --- |
| ![[hexagon_white_num_1.svg\|30]] | [[FRGHZ_CH1_Episode-Location\|LOCATION]]<br>Generate a region and specific place in the Forge |
| ![[hexagon_white_num_2.svg\|30]] | [[FRGHZ_CH1_Episode-Characters\|CHARACTERS]]<br>Generate inhabitants and non-player characters
| ![[hexagon_white_num_3.svg\|30]] | [[FRGHZ_CH1_Episode-Drama\|DRAMA]]<br>Generate actions that propel and start the episode |

You’ll need some oracles from Starforged to define some of these details, so make sure you have those on hand as well. Any time an oracle in Starforged is referenced you will see it underlined like this.

You don’t need to use all the prompts you generate. Include characters and details as needed. These tables are here to inspire each episode and help your story hit the ground running.

## Examples
> ### STARTING A CAMPAIGN
> Michelle sits down to play Ironsworn Starforged. She doesn’t want to define all her truths and parts of the setting before hand, she justwants to get into the action and play. She uses the episodic adventures procedure to do this after she makes her character.
>
> First she gets [[FRGHZ_CH1_Episode-Location#Oracle Region|Outlands]] (32) for a region and then a [[FRGHZ_CH1_Episode-Location#Oracle Primary Location - Outlands|Location]] (42) for a specific place. She finds the [[_SF_CH5_LocationThemes#Oracle Location Theme Type|Location]] oracle in the Starforged rulebook and rolls 81, the location is a ruined place. Oh, interesting! Next she rolls for [[FRGHZ_CH1_Episode-Characters#Oracle Supporting Character - Outlands|characters]] and gets Supernatural (90). She makes a note to find [[FRGHZ_CH2_Supernatural-Creation|that oracle]]  in this supplement to detail that entity when she needs to.
>
> Finally Michelle rolls for [[FRGHZ_CH1_Episode-Drama#Oracle Drama|drama]] and gets Hunting (66). Maybe her character is hunting this supernatural horror? But why? She decides to roll on the [[SF_CH5_Core_Action-Theme#Oracle Action|Action]] + [[SF_CH5_Core_Action-Theme#Oracle Theme|Theme]] oracle in Starforged and gets a 13 and99, “Betray Passage.” Oh, her character is hunting this undead horror because in life it knew a secret passage through the Forge! What is this passage? Where does it lead? Michelle gets ready to make the [[SF_CH3_Swear an Iron Vow|Swear an Iron Vow]] move to answer these questions.

> ### MIDDLE OF A CAMPAIGN
> Sara is playing a daring thief who has already had her fair share or heists and close calls in the Forge. She has a spare hour and wants to see what her character might be up to, so she grabs her dice.
>
> First she gets a [[FRGHZ_CH1_Episode-Location|location]]. A result of 03 and 16 puts her character in Terminus in a settlement. She consults the Starforged oracles for [[SF_CH5_Settle-Creation|settlements]] and rolls a 99. It’s a settlement of tens of thousands, so it’s pretty big. She then rolls for two [[FRGHZ_CH1_Episode-Characters#Oracle Supporting Character - Terminus|characters]] and gets a 50 and 47. Both starships, oh! Sara knows what’s happening her character stole a ship and is being pursued by another! She’s ready to go!
