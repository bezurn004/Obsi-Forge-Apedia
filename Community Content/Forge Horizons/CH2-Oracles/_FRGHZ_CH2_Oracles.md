> ## CONTENTS

> ### ECUMENOPOLIS
> A planet completely covered in artificial structures, industrialization, and multi-level settlements. When playing Starforged and rolling for a planet, if you get doubles, make that planet an ecumenopolis.

> ### SUPERNATURAL ORACLES
> Supernatural horrors are found in the shadows of the Forge, in ruins, voids, and derelicts. It is assumed these horrors had a human appearance once, but now have several different alterations.

> ### PRECURSOR VAULTS
> Those few specimens that remain from their ancient civilizations that stretched across the Forge millennia before humanity arrived. These are likely to be long dead and buried, but perhaps not...
